# The Dry Country

A wild land with no town of its own: sand and grey scrub to a swimming horizon, salt pans that
glare, dry wells with the rope cut short. Nomads and caravans cross it, and everything in it is
after water.

## Where it lies

- The Dry Country has four seeds (latitude, longitude): (215, 10), (-20, 130), (-215, 175) and (20,
	-215), in the gaps between the towns' lands. It is the third largest land (1,346 cells, 13.2%
	of the map), in six tracts:
	- north (302 cells; latitude 125 to 250, longitude -35 to 55), hilly: 129 hill cells, between the
		Barrens and the Weald;
	- west (256 cells; latitude -60 to 95, longitude -250 to -155), west of Bris;
	- south-east (332 cells; latitude -250 to -135, longitude 100 to 250), south of Kalle;
	- round Endworld (146 cells; latitude -250 to -180, longitude -225 to -135) and a strip beside it;
	- east (55 cells; latitude -35 to 20, longitude 110 to 145), north of Kalle.
- Features are few: 210 hill cells, 17 peak cells, 14 lake cells and three ponds.
- Neighbours: the Salt Shoals, the Weald, the Barrens and the Diggings on its edges, the Ashlands round
	Endworld (see lands/salt-shoals.md, lands/diggings.md, lands/ashlands.md).
- "Between Bris and Kalle": the lore sets the nomads, hellhounds, wyverns and the water cactus "between Bris
	and Kalle", but Bris, Midworld and Kalle lie on one diagonal with the Midlands in the middle of it. The road is
	read loosely, off the hub (world/overview.md): the nearest dry tracts are the west one beyond Bris and the east
	one north of Kalle. See continuity.md.

## Underfoot

Sand and grey scrub run flat to a swimming horizon. A salt pan glares white beside the track with
bleached bones at its edge; a dry well stands by the road with its rope cut short; scorpions shelter under
the flat stones and you step round them; a ring of stones marks an old camp long since left; thorny scrub
catches at your legs, its stems grey with dust. Sand stings the eyes, pours into boots, and a dry bone clicks.

Fights open as the monster rises out of loose sand, from the scrub beside the well, over the crest of a dune,
at the edge of a salt pan. Edges read "sand banks rise in long ridges", "nomad camps are said to lie out
on the sand", "dry wells and bleached bones mark the track".

## Climate

The driest land: by day the sky is heat 45% of the time, overcast 8%, drizzle and fog 2% each, and sun
the rest (43%); mud lasts a fifth as long as in the Midlands (8 minutes). It never snows, not even on
the peaks: falling weather is rain. See world/weather-and-time.md.

## The battlefield

Loose Sand (field `sand`, wind 1): earth and wind stronger, water and frost weaker, everyone slipping.
"The nomads who cross the dry country wrap their faces and keep to short steps. They say the sand is an
enemy that never tires, and that it wins the long fight."

## Objects

- Water Cactus: a fat ribbed cactus with yellow flowers. 18% of fights under sun or heat, 12% otherwise.
	Split open, it heals the creature that is worst hurt and puts out its fire; fire burns it to a husk.
	"Nomads keep a cactus cut open for the children, and the old ones say a cactus has more sense than a
	well. The water is cold, which surprises everyone who has only tasted the sand." See
	things/battlefield-objects.md.

## Monsters and levels

Levels run from 21 to 51 across the six tracts (the median is 43). Lore homes (families in
monsters/beasts.md, monsters/drakes-and-dragons.md and monsters/others.md):

- Scorpions: Scorpion (5), "desert-bred, big as a dog and the colour of old tea", under rocks along the
	road south of Roma; Rogue Scorpion (7), a thief of the caravan camps south of Roma; Dark Scorpion (15), in the
	dry hills past Narcissa; Silver Scorpion (40), under the dunes beyond Hambry.
	Caravan drivers shake out their boots every morning.
- Mirage (11), a shimmer on the desert road that looks like water, a town or a friend waving; drivers
	mark where they last saw one on their maps and take another road.
- Hellhound (20), in pairs or packs on "the hot road between Bris and Kalle", smelt a mile off by
	its sulphur; Kalle's old soldiers keep a hellhound's tooth as a charm.
- Lesser Wyvern (32), over the open plains between Bris and Kalle, after herds and carts; Wyvern (33), on
	the cliffs above that road. Carters paint their wagons with pitch, which wyverns hate.

## People

Nomads and caravan folk, drivers, carters, salt traders, the pan-men of the salt pans at the mouth of the
Kalle road. See peoples/trades.md and peoples/roads-and-outlaws.md.

## From the land

- Nomad's Band: plaited camel hair and copper wire, "each knot tied by a different hand", worn on the upper
	arm; it keeps the wearer going a little longer before the legs give out.
- Weakening Dust: pale powder from the salt pans at the mouth of the Kalle road (see lands/diggings.md).
- Silver Scorpion shells: dealers in Endworld pay well for an intact one.
