# The Ashlands

The land at the world's end: grey ash that puffs at every step, black glass in shards, cracks with
a red glow in them, and a heat that comes in the afternoon. Endworld sits in the south-west corner
of the map, and the map stops there.

## Where it lies

- Endworld (-250, -250) is the corner. The Ashlands are the smallest land (576 cells, about 5.6% of
	the map) and the most scattered. Their tracts:
	- the corner round Endworld (155 cells; latitude -250 to -145, longitude -250 to -170);
	- the west edge (151 cells; latitude -45 to 50, longitude -250 to -170), with a lake of 17 cells;
	- the north edge (105 cells; latitude 185 to 250, longitude -60 to -10), with 15 peak cells;
	- 67 cells south of Narcissa (latitude -240 to -195, longitude -175 to -120) and two small tracts on
		the south-east edge.
- Hills are nearly a third of its cells (174 hill cells, 24 peak cells): the "volcanic hills rise black and steep".
	See lands/waters-and-heights.md.
- Neighbours: the Dry Country wraps Endworld itself (128 of the cells within 75 tiles are Dry
	Country); the Greymoor lies to the north-east (see lands/dry-country.md and lands/greymoor.md).
- Beyond the map's edge lie Endworld's sea-coast, lake and mountains (the Siren, the Leviathan,
	the Titan, the Red Dragon). The map draws none of them. See continuity.md.

## Underfoot

Grey ash puffs up at every step, black glass glints in it, and the ground ticks as it cools. The
air bends in the heat; red glow shows in the cracks of the cinder fields; black glass lies in shards
"sharp as a knife"; a lava vent has cooled to a crust of black stone beside the track; dead burnt
trunks stand in the ash, "grey to the root". A vent hisses and falls quiet, a crust of glass cracks
under the boot, hot ash sifts into a collar, and somewhere close "a new crack opens in the ground,
with a groan".

Fights open through drifting ash, on cooling cinders, beside a steaming crack, over a black ridge of
cinder. Edges read "the grey ash fields begin", "the cinder fields of Endworld", "glass cutters' pits are
dug into the ground", "a thin column of smoke rises from a vent".

## Climate

The hot land: by day the sky is heat 60% of the time, overcast 10%, drizzle and fog 2% each, and
sun the rest (26%); mud dries in half the time (10 minutes). It never snows: falling weather is
rain, on the peaks too. "The heat rolls in from the edge of the world in the afternoon and is gone
again by dusk"; Roma's people say it is what Endworld breathes out. See world/weather-and-time.md.

## The battlefield

Cinders (field `cinders`, wind 1): fire stronger and burning hotter, frost weaker, wind stronger,
everyone a little unsteady. "The people of Endworld tie a wet cloth over the mouth before a fight.
They say a man who fears fire should stay out of the ash, and that the ash will find him anyway."
Endworld's scouts carry wet sacks as a habit. A hill, peak or lake takes its own field.

## Objects

- Steam Vent: a split in the black cinders breathing yellow steam. 15% of fights on cinders. It
	hisses a turn before it scalds one creature, and water sets it off at once. "The people of
	Endworld learn to hear" the hiss; Endworld's masons pile rubble on the cracks they can reach.
	The Aqua Elemental quenches vents on you. See things/battlefield-objects.md.

## Monsters and levels

Endworld's band is 49 to 50 (the edge reaches 51, and no monster is above 50), so the corner is the top of the
map; across the corner tract the levels run from 36 up to that top. The far ring of the map, every edge, has
these levels, not only the Ashlands. Lore homes (families in monsters/elementals.md,
monsters/demons.md, monsters/drakes-and-dragons.md, monsters/giants.md, monsters/beasts.md
and monsters/undead.md; the strongest in legends/upper-echelon.md):

- Fire things: Fire (24), a living flame in the ash fields where old battles left the ground warm;
	Fire Wyrm (32), in the lava vents under the hills near Endworld, up at night to burn the
	grazing land; Fire Raven (9), nesting in the scorched hills; Blaze (31) and Flame (28), fires
	loose from mills and forges.
- Daemons and the court: Red Daemon (48), riding the lava veins under the volcanic hills; Greatest
	Devil (43), its name painted red on the gates; Demon Prince (47), on the roads beyond Endworld;
	Demon Lord (48), in a hall of black glass beyond it; Demon King (49) and Black Daemon (50), road and
	realm "beyond the cracks".
- Giants and dragons: Titan (50), asleep in the mountains above Endworld; Red Dragon (41), on a
	hoard beneath the mountains past it; Colossus (49), a statue of stone and iron guarding the road.
- The dead and the afraid: Terror (31), at the crossroads where the gallows stood; Fear (23), on the
	road to Endworld; Knight (30), on the road from Gilead; Lycanthra (30), taking men's shapes near
	Endworld; Silver Slime (30), in the old sewers.
- The garrison's deserter: Renegade (42), a sergeant who took his squad over the wall when the pay
	stopped for four months.
- Beyond the edge: Siren (50), of the sea-coast; Leviathan (46), of the deep lake; Lucifuge (50), of the
	deepest pits.

## People

Masons, scouts, wardens, mercenaries, merchants, obsidian cutters, storm-watchers, the sages and
mages of Endworld, a mountain clan. See peoples/trades.md. Roads and deserters:
peoples/roads-and-outlaws.md.

## From the land

- Obsidian: black glass from "the cooling fields beyond Endworld"; cutters knap it into blades.
- Starsteel Sword: iron from a meteor that fell on the Endworld plain "before anyone lived there".
- Bearclaw Totem: from bears of the mountains above Endworld, kept by the clans; Shepherd's Lamp
	from the high valleys beyond Endworld.
- Seraph's Love and Seraph's Truth, from seraphs in the halls under Endworld; Demon's Hate, from a
	demon out of Endworld's cellars; Dragon's Tear, sold by Endworld's priests; Storm Lance, strung
	by the storm-watchers.
