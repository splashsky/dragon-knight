# The Copper Downs

Rolling hill country round Roma, wheat-coloured in grass and green-stained in its copper
roofs. Goblins hold the hills, drovers and farmers hold the valleys, and it rains.

## Where it lies

- Roma (30, 30) is its heart. The home tract runs from latitude 0 to 130 and longitude -45 to
	95 (335 cells), north and east of Midworld's farms; about 8.6% of the map in all.
- It is the hilliest land on the map: over half of its cells are hills or peaks, and in the home
	tract over a third are hills and nearly a quarter peaks. The largest range north of Roma
	(latitude 35 to 110, longitude -5 to 60) is the "cliffs and caves north of Roma", and it is
	one of the three highest on the map. A long lake (30 cells, in a patch of farmland) lies 20 to
	35 tiles north-east of Roma. See lands/waters-and-heights.md.
- Elsewhere the same country comes back in pockets of like climate: a big tract far south-east
	of Kalle (latitude -205 to -110, longitude 60 to 165), and patches among the Diggings and
	the Midlands; the high country above Kalle is partly Downs.
- Neighbours: the Midlands to the south-west, the Salt Shoals to the west, the Dry Country to
	the north and a stand of the Weald to the east (see lands/hambry-weald.md).

## Underfoot

Wheat-coloured grass over the downs; chalk tracks worn white by carts; gravel on the hill roads;
stone walls cutting the slopes into small fields with sheep behind them; a glassworks' smoke
over a ridge; a copper roof glinting green below; an old chapel on the next rise, "its bell
long silent". The turf is springy and gives under the heel; loose gravel skitters downhill.

Fights open on slopes: a drop off a wall into the track, a hollow with a cold fire, the hill
road where the carts turn. A goblin whistle sounds from a far ridge. Edges read "rolling downs,
gold with grass" and "goblin camps are said to lie in the hills".

## Climate

Dawn mist (10% more fog at dawn), a little heat (5% by day), more drizzle (10%) and cloud
(22%) than the Midlands, and mud that lasts half again as long (30 minutes). "Rain is the
common weather of Roma and Bris, and most farmers there would not know what to do with a dry
year." The street children of Roma say the mud is theirs by right. Roma's people shut
their shutters in the heat and say it is what Endworld breathes out. Rain comes in soft from the
west and the mud is slow to leave. See world/weather-and-time.md.

## The battlefield

Plain downs have no field of their own. A hill brings Rough Ground, a peak High Peaks, a pond
or lake Shallows. The goblins "set their feet on the bare rock and not the scree", and the
shepherds say the man on the higher side has the better of it.

## Objects

- Campfire: a ring of blackened stones and a few red coals, in every goblin camp and most of
	the drovers' yards out of Roma. 10% of fights on the Downs (18% at night), never in rain,
	snow or shallows. A spark may set a barrel beside it alight; goblins say a barrel beside a
	camp "is asking for trouble". The washerwomen of Roma keep a bucket by the door.
- Goblins carry blasting powder (see things/battlefield-objects.md).

## Monsters and levels

Roma's band is 5 to 7; across the home tract levels run mostly from 7 to 23 (the median is 16).
Lore homes (families in monsters/drakes-and-dragons.md, monsters/beasts.md and
monsters/others.md):

- Goblin (10), "in the hills along the road out of Roma", raiding caravans and running before
	the guards come. The Goblin Shaman (5) is the one to kill first; it is never met at random.
- Wyrm (28), in the caves under the cliffs north of Roma; Hellcat (18), on the rocky hills
	between Roma and Bris; Drakelor (3), on the road between Roma and Bris; Blaze (31), a fire that
	has been seen working along the road from Roma to Bris.
- Nisse (19), in barns and under farmhouse floors round Roma, kept sweet with a bowl of milk.
- Serafiend (38), guarding the old chapels in the hills above Roma, "where the bells no longer
	ring and nobody has asked why".
- Illusion (5), in "the mists of the marshes past Roma"; Spirit (18), in the ruins outside Roma;
	Dybbuk (9), which the old women of Roma say takes someone with an unpaid family debt.
- Scorpion (5) and Rogue Scorpion (7), desert-bred strays on the road south of Roma and in the
	caravan camps there. The Dry Country does not reach Roma on the map; see lands/dry-country.md
	and continuity.md.
- Bears come down from the hills above Roma in autumn (the Call Bear lore); the hunters of Roma
	know the wolf.

## People

Copper workers, glassmen, clockmakers, ironmongers, drovers, washerwomen, snake-catchers, the
Mages' guild and the lamplighters' guild. See peoples/trades.md. Training yards in Roma keep the
mud fresh to teach Mire; its taverns teach Craze. Roads and caravans: peoples/roads-and-outlaws.md.

## From the land

- Magic Stone: cloudy violet stone in the gravel of the hill roads round Roma; the Mages' guild
	buys it in bulk.
- Life Rock: pale-veined rock from the goblin camps in the hills past Roma; goblins wear them.
- Tinker's Buckle: leftover pitted brass that goblins use as belt plates.
- Angel's Joy: the one feather left when a winged guardian falls near Roma.
- Memory Drop: glass bead taken off monsters, bought by Roma's scholars.
