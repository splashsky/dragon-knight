# The Diggings

Kalle's country: old mines and new, red-brown scree, ore carts, and above it all a high country
of passes and snow. It is dry and bright, with no lakes, and it is where the blasting powder is
kept.

## Where it lies

- Kalle (-100, 100) is south-east of Midworld, on the same diagonal as Bris. The home tract is
	936 cells (latitude -250 to 45, longitude 10 to 250): the whole south-east quarter of the map,
	about 10.7% of it all. It is the second hilliest land (295 hill cells, 109 peak cells) and has
	no lakes at all and only four ponds.
- The high country above Kalle is the largest range on the map (156 peak cells, latitude -170 to
	-45, longitude 35 to 160), half Diggings and half Downs, and one of the three highest. The
	"passes above Kalle", "the Kalle peaks", the "snow-fields above Kalle" and the cliffs near
	Kalle are all this. A second range lies south of Midworld (latitude -160 to -130, around
	longitude 0). See lands/waters-and-heights.md.
- Other tracts: a patch south of Midworld (latitude -155 to -110, longitude -35 to 10) and a
	patch near the far south-east edge.
- Neighbours: the Midlands to the north-west, stands of the Copper Downs inside it, the Thornbog
	to the east and south-west, the Dry Country to the south (see lands/thornbog.md and
	lands/dry-country.md).

## Underfoot

Spoil heaps and red-brown scree crowd the track. A lamp burns at a timbered mine mouth; ore carts
squeal below; dust hangs and the rock rings under a boot. Timber props hold up an old adit;
rusted rails run along the track to a collapsed shaft; broken rock is stacked, sorted by size;
the hillside is cut in terraces of scree; ruined shrines stand on the rocks. A cold breath comes
up a shaft, a pick somewhere stops and listens, grit falls from the roof of a cutting.

Fights open between two spoil heaps, at a mine mouth, up a ladder out of the lower workings, on
the rail track between two ore carts, in a ruined shrine's doorway.

## Climate

Dry and bright: sun 45%, overcast 15%, drizzle 4%, a little heat (4%), mud that dries sooner
(16 minutes). The snow line is the usual latitude 150, so only the peaks snow: a pass above Kalle has
snow on the road half the year and the lowland has none. Storms run from Midworld to Gilead and
never come near Kalle, yet Kalle's wire men carry their spark rings out to the storm road to the north-west, "where the
lightning comes down": see continuity.md. See world/weather-and-time.md.

## The battlefield

No ground field of the Diggings' own. Its fights are Rough Ground on a hill and High Peaks on a
peak: "the herders of the passes beyond Kalle climb with a rope, and they say a fight on a ledge
goes to whoever keeps his feet. The dragons of the Kalle peaks breathe frost."

## Objects

- Explosive Barrel: a squat keg of blasting powder with a rag fuse. 7% of fights on plain
	Diggings ground, never in rain, downpour, thunderstorm, blizzard or shallows. Miners "buy
	blasting powder by the keg" and haul it in the cart with their ropes and bread; each has a
	story of a keg that went off beside a cook fire. Sold in Kalle, Narcissa and Gilead for 180
	gold. See things/battlefield-objects.md for what it does and who rolls them. A Lit Fuse is
	what the miners count under their breath.

## Monsters and levels

Kalle's band is 19 to 21; across the home tract levels run from 15 to 51 (the median is 34).
Lore homes (families in monsters/undead.md, monsters/beasts.md, monsters/drakes-and-dragons.md
and monsters/demons.md):

- The dead: Shade (3), in the ruined huts north of Kalle after dark; Megaskeleton (13), down the
	old roads dragging a cart of bones; Figment (19), in the shuttered houses at night; Polgergeist
	(25), at inns on the road out of Kalle; the Liche King (44) and the Liche Lord (42), whose court
	meets in the crypts under Kalle.
- Beasts: Nightshade (6), in shady glades and forest edges; Dark Wolf (17), on the forest road
	past Kalle; Werewolf (17), at the village edges, farmers bar their doors; Werebear (26) on
	the wooded slopes between Kalle and Narcissa; Lycanthor (28), in the hill country past Kalle.
	The woods round Kalle are timber on the slopes, not the Weald's Woods field (see continuity.md).
- Knights: Armor Knight (33), on the old barracks road east of Kalle; Pale Knight (39), bleached
	white in the snow-fields above it; Insurgent (46), whose farmers and smiths hold the hill roads
	above Kalle against the crown's road tax.
- Dragons: Blue Dragon (39) in the cold peaks above Kalle, young dragons on the cliffs near
	Kalle (the Dragon's Scale).
- Scoundrel (12), selling false maps on the roads out of Kalle.
- The pits under the old mines (Fiend, Lesser Devil, Devil, Greater Devil, Cherufiend, the
	stairs down from the mines) are taken to lie under the Diggings: see continuity.md.

## People

Miners and mine captains, pan-men at the salt pans, gem cutters, a cold-smith, armourers,
gladiators and their pit master, wire men, trappers and herders. See peoples/trades.md. Roads and
the bandits on them: peoples/roads-and-outlaws.md.

## From the land

- Ruby: red stones from the old mines near Kalle, traded for bread and boots.
- Magic Rock: dark-blue rock from the ruined shrines past Kalle.
- Weakening Dust: pale powder from the salt pans at the mouth of the Kalle road, mixed with a
	bitter white root the pan-men dig at night.
- Dragon's Scale: shed by a young dragon on the cliffs near Kalle.
- Rime Ring: grey iron from "a seam under the ice" that Kalle's miners wear under their gloves.
- Rimefur Coat: sewn in Kalle from the winter pelts of the high country.
- Cave bears (the Frost Staff's lore) are met in the mines' back passages.
