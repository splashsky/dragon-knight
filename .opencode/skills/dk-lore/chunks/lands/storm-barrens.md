# The Storm Barrens

Gilead's country, in the far north-west: broken walls, fallen towers, crypts and old
battlefields in grass, with a wind that never stops. Every storm that forms near Midworld
ends here, and the sky shows it.

## Where it lies

- Gilead (200, -200) sits in the north-west corner. The home tract is 724 cells (latitude 50 to 250,
	longitude -250 to -55), the whole north-west quarter of the map; the land is about 9.0% of it (920
	cells).
- Outliers: a patch of 80 cells in the north-east interior (latitude 110 to 165, longitude 60 to
	105), a patch of 61 cells south of Hambry, a corner of the far north-east edge.
- Water and height: a lake of 31 cells on the west edge (latitude 105 to 140, longitude -250 to
	-220), 22 pond cells, 67 hill cells and a range of 11 peak cells about 55 tiles east of
	Gilead, one of the highest spots on the map. See lands/waters-and-heights.md.
- Neighbours: the Salt Shoals and the Thornbog to the south-east; to the east a stand of the Weald
	and the Dry Country, then an ash field (see lands/salt-shoals.md, lands/thornbog.md,
	lands/hambry-weald.md, lands/dry-country.md, lands/ashlands.md).
- "Past Gilead" and "beyond Gilead" in the lore mean the last 50 tiles of the map's north-west
	corner.

## Underfoot

A broken wall runs off into the grass, its gate long fallen. Stones from a fallen tower lie
half buried; the barred doors of a ruined crypt have rusted shut; old bones and rust lie where
some battle ended; flooded mine workings lie in a hollow, black and still. The wind never stops
and it smells of rain a mile away. Crypts and chapels lie half hidden in the grass, and salt flats
lie "white and flat".

Fights open over an old wall, on cracked flagstones in the wind, among the broken stones of a crypt,
through a breach, under the walls of Gilead, beside a flooded mine shaft. In the lulls: the wind tugs
at friend and foe alike, thunder mutters "from a sky you left behind", a vulture settles on a broken
gatepost to watch, a dry bone cracks underfoot.

## Climate

Grey and windy: fog 6% (5% more at dawn), drizzle 8%, overcast 30%, less sun (20%). The snow line is
latitude 100, so most of the Barrens snow. Storms rain a step harder here: a squall's core is a
thunderstorm, and nothing falls harder than a thunderstorm (a blizzard where it snows). "Gilead gets the worst" of the
blizzards, and Gilead's wardens buy storm charms "before every season of high winds". See
world/weather-and-time.md for the storms' road.

## The battlefield

No ground field of its own, but wind is the point: a gale tears banners and fells dead trees.
Hills, peaks, lakes and ponds bring their own fields (see lands/waters-and-heights.md).

## Objects

- War Banner: a torn red-and-black standard on a broken lance. 8% of fights, not in a thunderstorm or
	blizzard. Everyone on the side it flies over deals 25% more damage while it stands. "The Storm
	Barrens tear more banners down than any enemy does," and the ballads say a standard that falls takes
	the courage with it. A knight raises one on his first turn (War Standard, which nobody sells).
- Dead Tree: 6% of fights, 25% in a gale. Watchmen on the Gilead road tell travellers to stand clear of
	the old dead trees while a gale blows.
- Explosive Barrels are sold in Gilead.
See things/battlefield-objects.md.

## Monsters and levels

Gilead's band is 39 to 41; across the home tract levels run from 30 to 51 (the median is 44).
Lore homes (families in monsters/undead.md, monsters/demons.md, monsters/giants.md, monsters/people.md
and monsters/beasts.md):

- The dead: Ultraskeleton (16), in the crypts past Gilead; Megawraith (21), fat on a battlefield's
	dead; Necromancer (26), in the old graveyards; Doomer (32) and Megadoomer (34) on the roads near
	Gilead; Horror (27), standing in a road until a lantern is lit.
- The garrison's ghosts: Strong Knight (35), who was told he could not hold the gate; Dark Knight (43)
	on the high passes past Gilead; Knight (30) on the road between Gilead and Endworld.
- Dawkare (29), choosing narrow passes (Gilead's guard captains warn recruits); Brute (27) and Large
	Brute (29), hired as caravan muscle.
- Beasts and giants: Beast (23), in the far hills near Gilead; Dark Bear (24), in the deep woods past
	Gilead; Ogre (23), in the hills east of it; Giant (43), in the high valleys beyond it.
- Wind, water and wing: Aqua Elemental (31), in the flooded mines; Shimmer (8), over the salt flats in
	the hottest weeks; Greatest Wyvern (37), on the ridges past Gilead; Silver Slime (30), in a few wet
	cellars near Gilead.
- Demons and the like: Demon (45) tumbled off the walls and fed in the ruins past Gilead; Archfiend
	(42), bound in the north until the seal cracked; Dark Daemon (49), in the deepest ruins; Serafiend
	(38), a seraph of the high passes beyond Gilead.
- Magician (8), thrown out of a tower near Gilead for practising on the students; Demagogue (48) and
	Nihilist (47), of Gilead's squares and archives.

## People

The garrison and its wardens, priests and temple smiths, glassmiths and glassblowers, smiths, horse
traders, bog priests, a family of elixir makers, furriers. See peoples/trades.md. The roads and the
crown's deserters: peoples/roads-and-outlaws.md.

## From the land

- Vulture Plume: from a vulture "as big as a farm cart" that followed the armies for a generation.
- Void Flask: black glass, blown in Gilead from sand miners bring up "from below the salt marsh".
- Glass Blade, Sunsteel Mail, Dawn Mace, Tower Shield, War Sabre, Diamond: Gilead's smiths and glassmiths.
- Demon's Fall and Demon's Sin: from demons of the walls and the ruins. Seraph's Joy: ember from a
	seraph in the passes beyond Gilead.
- Elixir: made by one family's workshop in Gilead.
