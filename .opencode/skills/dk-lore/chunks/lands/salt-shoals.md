# The Salt Shoals

The grey tidal flats round Bris, with cliffs, ruined towers and hills behind them. It is the
wettest and mistiest of the towns' lands and the one where a fight is most likely to be
in water.

## Where it lies

- Bris (70, -70) is its heart, north-west of Midworld. The home tract is 677 cells (latitude
	-35 to 180, longitude -165 to 10), about 7.6% of the map, running west of Midworld's farms and
	up toward the north. It is hilly: 179 hill cells, 12 peak cells, and lakes and ponds in
	plenty (38 lake cells).
- Water: a lake 30 tiles north of Bris (latitude 100, longitude -75) and, further north-west
	toward Gilead, the biggest lake on the map (81 cells, half Thornbog and a third Shoals). See
	lands/waters-and-heights.md.
- Outliers: strips at the far east and south edges of the map, near the Thornbog.
- Neighbours: the Midlands to the south-east and the Copper Downs to the east, the Thornbog to the
	north and north-west, the Dry Country to the north-east (see lands/thornbog.md and
	lands/dry-country.md), and the Storm Barrens further to the north-west.
- There is no open sea on the map (the plan's decision 4). The Shoals are flats, channels, pools,
	lakes and ponds, and the "tide line" is as much sea as the game draws.

## Underfoot

Grey flats to the tide line, with shells that crunch and crack at every step. Salt on the lips
and the smell of weed. Nets hang drying on stakes, pearl divers' stakes stand in rows across the
wet sand, rock pools hold small crabs and the odd dull shell, weed lies in stinking heaps along the
tide line, and water seeps up through the sand where you stand. Gulls complain; a bell buoy
clangs somewhere out of sight; the tide hisses in; a crab goes sideways into a pool.

Fights open among the stakes, between shallow channels, up a shell bank "dripping weed", among
wrecked boats on the sand. Edges read "grey salt flats", "salt marsh and shell banks", "cliffs and
ruined towers rise above the flats", "the tidal flats of Bris".

## Climate

Sea mist at dawn (25% more fog at dawn, 5% at night, 6% by day), drizzle 12%, overcast 25%, less
sun (30%), mud lasting a quarter longer (25 minutes). "Rain is the common weather of Roma and
Bris." Carters say a wet road is worse than a long one. See world/weather-and-time.md.

## The battlefield

The Shoals have no ground field of their own: it is a plain fight on the flats unless a lake
or a pond is underfoot (Shallows: water and lightning stronger, fire weak and unable to catch,
everyone wading slowly) or a hill or peak (Rough Ground, High Peaks). "The pearl divers of Bris
wade out to the shell beds like this every day, and they say a diver who is afraid of the water
is the first to drown."

## Objects

- Tide Pool: a rock pool of brine, crabs and weed. 12% of fights on the Shoals (6% on any
	Shallows), never in snow or blizzard. Broken, it soaks everyone on the field, objects
	too: fires and fuses go out, lightning bites deeper. Divers step round them (the crabs have
	claws to spare), boys throw stones at them for a bet, and the fishwives of Bris call a splash
	"the cheapest cure for a burning cloak". The status is Soaked: "a wet man is hard to burn and
	easy to shock".

## Monsters and levels

Bris's band is 13 to 15; across the home tract the levels run from 11 to about 37 (the median is
24). Lore homes (families in monsters/drakes-and-dragons.md, monsters/elementals.md and
monsters/beasts.md):

- Drakemal (6), a heavy black drake in the caves in the hills near Bris; Shadow Raven (6), over the
	ruined towers near Bris after dusk.
- Terra Elemental (25), in the old quarries and mine galleries east of Bris.
- Flame (28), a fire loose from a forge or burning barn; "smiths in Bris keep a barrel of water by
	the door for them".
- Grey Wolf (13), following the caravans out of Bris; Hellcat (18) and Drakelor (3) on the road
	to Roma.
- Wyverns nest on the cliffs above the Bris road, and a young dragon on the cliffs above Bris
	gave the Dragon's Claw; the Bris-Kalle road itself is in lands/dry-country.md.

## People

Pearl divers, fishermen and fishwives, smiths, a tinsmith and a silversmith, the big apothecary,
clerics and the priests and paladins of Bris, innkeepers, carters, caravan guards and beekeepers.
See peoples/trades.md and peoples/roads-and-outlaws.md. Bris's guards pay a bounty for drakelor
claws, which are sharp enough to serve as needles.

## From the land

- Pearl: pulled from the shell beds south of Bris with a rope and a knife; the shallow beds are
	picked clean every spring; mages grind them into ink.
- Tortoise Shell: plates from a swamp tortoise nearly as big as a cart, cut into squares and
	lashed to a harness by Bris fishermen.
- Life Stone: rough red-tinted stone under the boulders on the roads round Bris.
- Hornet Sting: the barb of a marsh hornet, sold by the beekeepers east of Bris.
- Dragon's Claw (young dragon, cliffs above Bris); Angel's Love (a guardian that fell to a
	hunting party out of Bris).
