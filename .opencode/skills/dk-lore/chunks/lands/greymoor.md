# The Greymoor

Narcissa's country: grey-purple heather to every horizon, black peaty water, standing stones
older than any road, and fog. It is the foggiest land. The foundries of Narcissa glow under the
cloud at night, and out on the moor the dead are not tidy.

## Where it lies

- Narcissa (-130, -130) is south-west of Midworld. The home tract is 955 cells (latitude -250 to
	10, longitude -250 to -15): the whole south-west quarter of the map. Counting its outliers the
	Greymoor is the second largest land (about 13.8% of the map, 1,406 cells).
- It has outliers in the north-east: five patches among Hambry's forest (about latitude 85 to 250,
	longitude 110 to 250, the largest 136 cells). These are Hambry's "high pastures", "grey hills" and
	"bare ridges"; see lands/hambry-weald.md.
- Water and height: 86 lake cells (a lake of 34 cells lies 45 to 100 tiles west of Narcissa), 26
	pond cells, 161 hill cells and 21 peak cells. See lands/waters-and-heights.md.
- Neighbours: the Midlands to the north-east, the Salt Shoals to the north, the Dry Country and the
	Ashlands along the west edge and in the south-west corner, and patches of the Weald and the
	Thornbog among it (see lands/ashlands.md, lands/dry-country.md, lands/thornbog.md).

## Underfoot

Grey-purple heather, cut by black peaty water; cotton grass nodding on wet ground; peat cuttings
stacked and black beside the path; old cairns and boundary stones that the path bends round; a
standing stone leaning over the way; the rusted ribs of an old siege engine half sunk in the
heather. Peat crumbles under the heel and water comes up through the cracks. A curlew cries; bells
from Narcissa drift over the heather; something splashes in a pool and goes quiet.

Old battlefields lie grassed over under the heather. Fights open from behind a leaning stone or a
heather bank, from a peat pool "black water running off it", beside the siege engine. Edges read
"the grey moor", "the heather of the Greymoor begins", "standing stones line the moor road", "the
kilns and foundries of Narcissa smoke".

## Climate

The grey land: fog 6%, and half the dawns (50% more at dawn, 15% more at night), drizzle 10%,
overcast 30%, sun 25%, mud half again as long (30 minutes). "Fog comes off the low ground about
Narcissa at dawn and stays until the sun is well up." Carters wait at the crossroads for it to lift
and the innkeepers sell them cheap ale; the bandits of Narcissa call a morning fog the best time
of the year for business. See world/weather-and-time.md.

## The battlefield

No ground field of its own. Hills, peaks and lakes bring theirs (see lands/waters-and-heights.md).
The footing is boggy in the low ground and good gravel on the river banks.

## Objects

- Standing Stone: a lichen-grey menhir leaning over one side of the field. 8% of fights on the
	Greymoor, 14% in fog. "Nobody living knows who raised the stones, and the shepherds who graze among
	them have stopped guessing." The herders keep out from under the lean; Narcissa's mages swear an
	arcane bolt tips one over, the shepherds say a tremor will. It never moves by itself.
- Dead Tree: a bare snag at the moor's edge. 6% of fights, 25% in a gale (a thunderstorm or a
	blizzard). Woodcutters call them widowmakers.
- Explosive Barrels are sold in Narcissa (see lands/diggings.md for their home).

## Monsters and levels

Narcissa's band is 25 to 27; across the home tract levels run from 16 to 51 (the median is 33).
Lore homes (families in monsters/undead.md, monsters/giants.md, monsters/beasts.md,
monsters/drakes-and-dragons.md and monsters/demons.md):

- The dead: Ghoul (7), on the old battlefields near Narcissa; Liche (35), Liche Master (38) and
	Liche Prince (40), of the dead school beyond Narcissa, with its stolen chained books; Shadow Dragon (44) on the roads
	outside Narcissa after dusk.
- The hills past Narcissa: Dawk (19), Dawkor (26) and Dawkare (29) in caves and old mines and passes;
	Orc (15) and Uruk (20) in their own valleys; Brute (27) and Large Brute (29) at the pass; Wizard
	(20), in ruined towers; Dark Scorpion (15) under stones in the dry hills.
- Cold uplands: Frost Raven (7), north of Narcissa; Frost Wyrm (30), in the passes above Narcissa in
	deep winter; ice wolves high on the passes (the Frost Fang).
- Fire: Fira Elemental (34), on scorched ground near Narcissa; Fire Raven (9), nesting in scorched
	hills.
- Nightmare (33), at inns beyond Narcissa; Incubus (44) at inns on the road to Narcissa;
	Demons (45) are watched for by bell-ringers in the towns near it; Lycan (25), on the road
	between Narcissa and Hambry.
- Black Dragon (47), over "the marshes beyond Narcissa" (see lands/thornbog.md).

## People

Foundry men, potters and kilns, glass-cutters, the mages of the college and their stills, gate
guards, watchmen, judges, infirmary healers, shepherds and herders. See peoples/trades.md. Roads, inns
and bandits: peoples/roads-and-outlaws.md.

## From the land

- Topaz: yellow-gold stone from the river gravel near Narcissa, prized by thieves and archers.
- Cinder Blade and Cinder Shield: from Narcissa's foundries and the kiln that fires its roof tiles.
- Frost Fang: lost by ice wolves high on the passes past Narcissa.
- Dragon's Tooth: from a dragon killed by a hunting party out of Narcissa.
- Shade Charm: black glass cut after dark, from stone said to come from a cave under the north ice.
- Guard Tonic: grey oily tonic the gate guards drink before a night watch.
