# The upper echelon

## What counts

Monsters from level 42 up, and the dragons (the green one, level 36, and on). The rest of the world is
ordinary life, and these are what the ordinary tell stories about. They are met on the outer rings: Gilead's
gate already meets the dragons (levels 39 to 41), and Endworld meets 49 and 50 at the edge
(`world/overview.md`).

The legendary gear is what they leave. A drop is the gift of a level, not of a beast: any monster of
the right level or above may leave one, 1 in 30 victories, and the tales only say where the first was
found. The numbers are in `things/legendary-sets.md`.

## The old story

As the lore tells it, in order:

1. **Before the towns**: the Titan "remembers the first towns being built"; the Black Dragon has been seen
	over the marshes "since before the towns were walled". The Demon King "rules the realm beyond the cracks
	and has done so since before the first town was founded at Midworld".
2. **The cracks.** Demons "came up through the cracks in the old roads": soldiers first (the Demon), then
	daemons, devils and the Greatest Devil, who is obeyed by the others. Their stronghold is a hall of black
	glass beyond Endworld, ruled by the Demon Lord; the Demon Prince sends them forward in waves.
	The devils' own pits lie under the old mines, in ranks: fiends, lesser devils, devils, the Cherufiends
	who hold the stairs, greater devils. Under them all is Lucifuge, "the fallen seraph who rules the deepest
	pits".
3. **The oath.** The Paladins keep "an oath sworn before the first dragon fell: to carry light into every dark
	place on the map". Dawn is "the Paladin's answer to the long wars the Demon King started", and the prayer
	is written in the oath book at the Midworld chapel.
4. **The old kingdom and its crown.** The Liche King was "a lord of the old kingdom who had himself bound to a
	throne of bone". The crown kept garrisons at Hambry, Gilead and Endworld, a guard (the Dark Knights, who
	swore a different oath), a road tax, and sergeants who went unpaid. Its old wars left the Colossus at the
	pass, the battlefields about Narcissa and past Gilead, and phantoms who stand guard over posts nobody pays.
5. **The stars.** A star fell at Midworld and was forged into the Destiny Blade; another fell on the Endworld
	plain "before anyone lived there" and gave the iron of the Starsteel Sword.
6. **Now.** Crowds in Gilead's squares are told the crown is starving them; the Insurgent holds the hill roads
	above Kalle; the Renegade tolls the road he guarded; the Traitor sells whatever gate comes next.

## The dragons

| Dragon | Level | Lair | Breath |
|---|---|---|---|
| Green | 36 | the marsh-forest beyond Hambry, under fog | venom |
| Blue | 39 | the cold peaks above Kalle | frost |
| Red | 41 | on a hoard beneath the mountains past Endworld | fire |
| Shadow | 44 | the roads outside Narcissa after dusk | shadow |
| Dark | 45 | a mountain under Hambry; wakes about once in a generation | shadow |
| Black | 47 | the marshes beyond Narcissa; "the oldest dragon known to the road-wardens" | venom and miasma |

The Dark Dragon "collects names instead" of gold. The Red Dragon "has never once been known to share a gold
piece". The Black Dragon "leaves a traveller alone if he leaves it something shiny, though nobody has tested
that lately". The Green Dragon "takes only the gold it is owed". Smaller kin are in
`monsters/drakes-and-dragons.md`.

Five of them (the Greatest Devil, Demon Prince, Demon Lord, Demon King and Red Daemon) carry Brimstone
into a fight. See `monsters/demons.md`. The incubus and succubus (44, 47) and the serafiends (38) are the
fallen and the tempters among them.

## The dead, the great and the crown's servants

- **Dead**: the Liche Lord (42), steward of the Liche King's crypts, and the Liche King (44), whose "court of
	the dead still meets in the crypts under Kalle, where the floors are cold and every chair is full". The
	Liche King leaves Miasma (`monsters/undead.md`).
- **Great things**: the Giant (43), the Behemoth (48), the Leviathan (46, "its back is sometimes taken for an
	island"), the Siren (50), the Colossus (49) and the Titan (50: "He does not hate men. He simply does not see them
	until they stand on his foot"). See `monsters/giants.md`.
- **Men**: the Renegade (42), the Dark Knight (43), the Traitor (45), the Insurgent (46), the Nihilist (47) and
	the Demagogue (48). Each was once somebody of the crown's world (`monsters/people.md`).

## The Destiny gear and the four sets

- **Destiny**: the Blade, the Aegis and the Raiment. "Said to have been forged in Midworld from a fallen star";
	the Aegis was "carried by the first knight to hold the road out of Midworld"; the Raiment is "woven for someone
	else first". The only shop that sells them is Endworld's (`towns/endworld.md`).
- **The Dragon's** (accessories): Scale and Claw from young dragons on the cliffs by Kalle and Bris; Plate from
	the scorched road past Hambry; Tooth from a hunting party out of Narcissa; Tear from a lair, sold by the priests
	of Endworld; Wing from a dragon brought down over the marshes beyond Gilead.
- **The Angel's**: feathers, a lens and a ring dropped by guardians that fall: Joy near Roma, Rise in the hills
	past Hambry, Truth (the judges of Narcissa keep a box), Love from a Bris hunting party.
- **The Seraph's**: embers and gold glass from seraphs slain "in the high passes beyond Gilead" (Joy), "over the
	ash plains" (Rise) and in "the fire-lit halls under Endworld" (Love, Truth).
- **The Demon's**: Sin (ruins past Gilead), Fall (a demon that tumbled off Gilead's walls), Lie (a demon in a
	merchant's voice outside Hambry), Hate (a demon out of the cellars of Endworld). Each gives strength and takes
	something.
- Thornwood gear is a set of its own, from the bog (`things/legendary-sets.md`).

## The late spells

The spells learned last are the strongest, and a class learns them at the end of its road:

- **Mage**: Gaia (70), Starfall (76, "cannot be bought in a shop"), Chaos (79), Plague (83), Inferno (88),
	Raise Golem (94) and Whiteout (100).
- **Paladin**: Radiance (58), Judgement (64), Dawn (90), Last Stand (96).
- **Warrior**: Fury (70), Venom (82), Curse (94) and Warlord (100).
- **Monsters only**: Doom (the Doomers), Deluge (the Leviathan, "nobody has ever seen a Deluge cast by a man"),
	Thunderbolt (the Liche Master) and the breaths. See `magic/mages.md`, `magic/paladins.md`, `magic/warriors.md`.

## How it sounds

Only these speak of the world as if it were a story. The rest, even the towns' own people, are plain. The
habits that recur around them (not answering a name, leaving something shiny) are in `legends/sayings.md`.
