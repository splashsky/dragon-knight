# Slimes

The humblest kin of the land, and the first every hero meets. Midworld's saying is that
slimes are what is left when a spell goes wrong; nobody has been able to show otherwise. The
Warrior's trade is learned on the road to Roma "where the slimes are many and the inns are
few" (see `magic/warriors.md`).

Levels 1 and 10 are ordinary life. A slime has no tells and no tricks: it hops and bites.
Where they are met follows their level (see `world/overview.md`): the blue and red kinds only
within a few steps of Midworld, the dark one around Bris, the silver one far out.

## The kin

- **Blue Slime** (`blue_slime`, level 1). A wobbling drop of blue jelly that hops along the
	roads near Midworld. Its bite barely stings, yet every hero remembers the first one they
	defeated. Wet jelly: water slides off it (resists water), and lightning shorts it out
	(weak to lightning).
- **Red Slime** (`red_slime`, level 1). A redder and crosser cousin, in the damp cellars and
	ditches around Midworld. It stings a little more and does not like being poked. Its jelly
	is warm: it resists fire, is weak to frost, and a Fire Flask cannot set it alight (it
	wards off burning). Its bite is fire. Cellar keepers swear that a pinch of salt sends one
	back down the drain, though it never works twice.
- **Dark Slime** (`dark_slime`, level 10). A slime that has lived too long in the cellars
	under old towns where no light reaches. Almost black, and it stings more; its bite is
	shadow. It resists shadow, and is weak to holy light and to fire. Cellar keepers salt the
	stairs against it, which works until the salt runs out.
- **Silver Slime** (`silver_slime`, level 30). A rare cousin with a bright, hard body: only
	15 hit points, but armor 200 and a bite of 100, so it is hard to hit and hits back. It is
	immune to the hurt spells (arcane), resists physical blows by half, and cannot be put to
	sleep, which is why nobody has found a spell that will hold it still. It is said to carry a
	pocketful of coins from the people it has swallowed. Lightning is its one weakness.
	Lore places it in the old sewers under Endworld and a few wet cellars near Gilead.

## What people say

- Cellar keepers salt their stairs for red and dark slimes; the salt is a habit more than a
	cure.
- Small drops come from slimes near Midworld: the Life Pebble, "a small grey pebble that
	slimes and rats leave behind" (see `things/legendary-sets.md`).
- Nobody has a good word for the silver kind except those who have caught one.
