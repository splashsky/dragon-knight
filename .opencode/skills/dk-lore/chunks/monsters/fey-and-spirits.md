# Fey and spirits

Things without a proper body: the dead who stayed, shapes of dark and light, and the small
folk of ponds, wind and barns. The bodies of the dead are in `monsters/undead.md`.

## How to deal with them

- **Hurt spells go through** shadows, shades, figments, illusions, mirages, shimmers and
	phantoms (immune to arcane: Hurt, Pain, Maim, Rend, Chaos, Magic Bolt; see `magic/elements.md`).
	Steel and the elements work. A spark disperses an illusion; torchlight burns a shadow; holy
	light weakens every restless dead.
- **The fey are weak to iron** (physical blows): the nymph, sylph and nisse take extra damage
	from steel. "Fey: iron hurts them."
- Ghosts, specters, phantoms, spirits, haunts and polgergeists cannot be poisoned or put to sleep.
- The old habits against them: a lamp in the window (shades; old wives of Kalle), salt on the
	stair or the bed (cellar slimes, the nightmare, the polgergeist), a bowl of milk (the nisse),
	a blessed grave (ghosts), a bell at the gate. Nobody can say that any of them work, and
	nobody stops.

## The restless dead

They died owing, or unpaid, or unavenged. The debt is the story: ghosts moan about one,
specters count theirs, spirits died owing one, the dybbuk is called by one. None of it is
collected. "Do not answer when one calls your name."

- **Ghost** (`ghost`, 6). The spirit of someone who died on the road between Midworld and Kalle
	and never quite left, drifting through old graveyards and ruined inns, moaning about a debt
	nobody remembers. Priests in Midworld bless a grave for a few gold, which keeps most ghosts
	quiet and some away. Resists shadow and physical blows a little.
- **Dybbuk** (`dybbuk`, 9). A restless spirit that wears a living body like a borrowed coat and
	will not give it back. Old women in Roma say it takes hold of someone with a debt unpaid to
	its family. Saps your strength before it hits. The body sleeps (half the time) but the spirit
	does not.
- **Specter** (`specter`, 14). The spirit of a moneylender who died still counting his debts,
	rattling coin that is no longer there in the old counting houses of the towns. Anyone who owes
	money to the dead is in for a nasty surprise.
- **Phantom** (`phantom`, 14). A soldier who died without his pay and still stands guard at the
	old barracks and ruined forts where nobody has been paid in years. Blades pass through it
	(half), Hurt does nothing, so you outlast it. Aches you with Pain and knits itself under half.
- **Spirit** (`spirit`, 18). The restless shade of someone who died owing a debt, drifting
	through the ruins outside Roma. It cannot be cut but it can drain warmth and memory (one
	Withering). Priests in Bris sell a blessing that makes them easier to hit, though they
	disagree on whether the spirits feel the difference. The player's own Call Spirit is the
	same kind of shade, bound for a few turns (`magic/allies.md`).
- **Haunt** (`haunt`, 22). A house-ghost clinging to the place where its death was never
	avenged, usually a ruined inn or manor outside Hambry. Fills the house with fog and throws
	Pain from within it. It is bound to its ground: burn the building or lay it with the right
	rites. The Hambry priests keep a whole book of them, most of it grim.
- **Polgergeist** (`polgergeist`, 25). Died angry and never properly buried; throws dishes,
	stones, furniture (Hurt, Pain, or a plain blow, no telling which). Innkeepers on the road out
	of Kalle pay a priest to walk the rooms with salt before a bed goes out for the night.

## Shadows and shapes of dark

- **Shadow** (`shadow`, 2). A patch of darkness that moves on its own, most often at dusk along
	the old walls of Midworld. Spells of harm slide off it, so mages rely on a blade; torchlight
	burns it. Gravediggers say it only follows people with something to hide.
- **Shade** (`shade`, 3). A shadow that has grown a body of sorts, in the ruined huts north of
	Kalle after dark. Weak to fire and to holy light. Old wives in Kalle keep a lamp in the
	window, and swear it keeps the shades away.

## Illusions and dream-things

Light, heat and sleep make them: they come in the hours and the weather when people see badly.

- **Illusion** (`illusion`, 5). A shape of light and suggestion in the mists of the marshes past
	Roma. Its blows are real though the figure is not. Old travellers say to ignore what you see
	and listen for the blow.
- **Shimmer** (`shimmer`, 8). Rippling light over the salt flats near Gilead, too bright to look
	at, in the hottest weeks: in heat or sun it dazzles and weakens. Salt traders say anyone who
	follows it is not seen again.
- **Mirage** (`mirage`, 11). A shimmer on the desert road that looks like water, a town, or a
	friend waving. Walk toward it and it moves off, then it comes for you. In heat or sun the
	thirst puts you to sleep. Caravan drivers mark on their maps where they last saw one and take
	a different road.
- **Figment** (`figment`, 19). A thing made from a dream someone had too often. It turns up in
	the shuttered houses of Kalle at night as a friend or a lost parent, and waits for a hero to
	come closer. Weakens you, now and then lulls you to sleep. Dreamers in Narcissa say a figment
	fades if you stop believing in it, "which is easier said than done".
- **Nightmare** (`nightmare`, 33). A horse-shaped thing with eyes like cooling coals, said to
	visit sleeping travellers in the inns beyond Narcissa. It brings the night on its first turns,
	casts Dream on its 1st, 4th, 7th turn, and rides the sleeper (Withering). Those who wake with
	bruises and no memory of the night blame it. Mages keep a charm of salt by the bed, and the
	wise say it helps.

## The fey

Small folk, quick to play and slow to forgive.

- **Nymph** (`nymph`, 12). A woodland spirit that sings from the edge of ponds and lures
	wanderers off the path; men who follow are found later, dry and sleepy, sitting by the water.
	Sings you to sleep, fires a water jet, heals under half. Nymphs do not fight well, but they
	have never needed to. Fishers say she only fires the jet at men who have been singing.
- **Sylph** (`sylph`, 15). A spirit of the wind, a thin shape in the mountain passes beyond
	Hambry; playful and cool. Blows hats off, scatters campfires, leads climbers toward cliffs for
	fun. Calls the rain and arcs a spark through it. Mages value the lightning they carry; sylphs
	value being left alone.
- **Nisse** (`nisse`, 19). A small bearded trickster in barns and under farmhouse floors around
	Roma. Left a bowl of milk it keeps the cows healthy; crossed, it sets the hay alight and
	steals boots from sleeping feet (sleep, then Ember, not in rain). This one is out on the
	roads, which the farmers take as a sign the bowls have stopped going out.

## Where they are met

Shadows and ghosts at the start (levels 2 to 6), the dead and the fey from Roma to Kalle
(levels 9 to 19), the haunts and the nightmare toward Narcissa and Hambry (22 to 33). The
places the lore names (Gilead, Hambry, Narcissa) are where the kind is told of; see
`continuity.md#where-monsters-live`.
