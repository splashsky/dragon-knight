# Demons and devils

Two infernal countries are told of. **The pits** lie under the old mines and under Hambry, where
devils keep ledgers and sign contracts. **The realm beyond the cracks** lies past Endworld,
where the demons hold their court and wage the long wars the Demon King started. Both break
through "the cracks in the old roads" from time to time. All of them are weak to holy light and
resist fire; most ward off burning in part or whole.

Their common field is **Brimstone**: sulphur and cinders come up through the floor and fire hits
harder, frost and holy light weaker (`magic/afflictions.md` for statuses, `magic/elements.md`
for the field). A Paladin's Consecrate drives it out. The Demon Prince, the Demon Lord, the
Demon King and the Greatest Devil all set it; none of them casts fire under its own aura.

Levels from 42 are the upper echelon (`legends/upper-echelon.md`).

## Imps

- **Scamp** (`scamp`, 4). A small mischievous imp in the woods and fields around Midworld, mostly
	for the trouble. Hides in hedges, ties boot laces together, laughs. Alone it is no danger; a
	pack empties a hungry traveller's bag in a minute. Two Hurts, then it only swings.
- **Imp** (`imp`, 12). A small winged devil that serves nobody for long: pranks on sleepers,
	hides the keys to inn doors, whispers bad prices to shopkeepers. Puts you to sleep, then
	sets your boots on fire. Not worth the killing, except that it will otherwise steal your boots.
- **Hellion** (`hellion`, 16). A red-skinned brawler from the fire pits beyond Hambry; fights for
	sport and drink rather than gold and challenges anyone who looks like a fighter. Wise
	travellers look like cooks when passing through. Sets you alight and rages under half life.

## The devils of the pits

A ladder, and they keep to it. Polite until the fight begins; the rules of the pit are more
binding than the rules of the road.

- **Fiend** (`fiend`, 37). The lowest, the first most adventurers meet, from the pits under the old
	mines. Fast, strong and dull, fighting for the pleasure of it. The skin is hot (its blows are
	fire), so wear gloves.
- **Lesser Devil** (`lesser_devil`, 37). Above the fiends and aware of it; runs errands and
	collects debts with a grin. Will bargain before it fights; a clever tongue may buy a way
	out, rarely a cheap one.
- **Devil** (`devil`, 40). Horns, a tail and a contract for every soul that comes near. Holds the
	middle ranks of the pits below the mines.
- **Greater Devil** (`greater_devil`, 41). A senior devil with a long red tail and a ledger that
	never closes; signs the contracts the lesser ones carry and keeps the accounts of every soul
	that ever bargained with the pits. Rarely leaves the deep stairs. Curses you from its second
	turn ("Interest accrues"), with now and then a lash of cinders.
- **Cherufiend** (`cherufiend`, 41). Once a guard of the pit gates and still stands like one,
	shield in one hand, chain in the other. Holds the stairs from the mines down to the deeper
	pits and checks every visitor's papers. Most adventurers fight their way through, which is
	what it expects. Resists physical blows a little.
- **Archfiend** (`archfiend`, 42). The thing a summoner's circle is meant to hold, and it has
	never cared for the circle. Scholars in Gilead say it was bound once in the north and walked
	out when the seal cracked. It fights with hands and hunger together; under half life its
	hunger shows (it drains you and hits harder). It has never learned to lose quietly.
- **Greatest Devil** (`greatest_devil`, 43). The strongest of the demons that came up through the
	cracks in the old roads, and the one the others obey. Its name is painted red on the gates of
	Endworld by people who did not want to be the next to hear it. Mages say it is bound to a
	single sigil. Sets the brimstone first, lashes with it, and below half life burns you.
- **Serafiend** (`serafiend`, 38). A seraph that fell out of the choir and kept its voice: words
	sweet at first and sour by the second sentence. Guards old chapels in the hills above Roma,
	where the bells no longer ring and nobody has asked why. Fights as a Paladin: prays
	(Dawnstrike) while strong, heals itself when hurt, and under half it curses you.

## The realm beyond the cracks

- **Demon** (`demon`, 45). The common soldiers of the realm, strong and quick but not clever,
	which is why the Prince sends them in front. They arrive first and in the greatest numbers.
	Towns near Narcissa keep a bell-ringer to sound the alarm.
- **Demon Prince** (`demon_prince`, 47). Commands the demons on the roads beyond Endworld and
	sends them forward in waves. Cleverer than his troops, he knows the names of the knights sent
	against him. He rarely fights himself; he watches from a height and counts the dead. He sets
	the brimstone and lashes from a height until he is hurt, then joins and hits harder.
- **Demon Lord** (`demon_lord`, 48). Rules a stronghold of demons beyond Endworld, the last mind
	between the Prince and the King. A hall of black glass; petitions from his captains by
	lamplight. His court takes a turn each: judgement (a curse), sentence (a rend), then
	execution, a plain blow. Under half life he calls his guard (brimstone), then guards. Those
	who reach him rarely leave unchanged.
- **Demon King** (`demon_king`, 49). Rules the realm beyond the cracks and has since before the
	first town at Midworld. A crown of cooled lava; never seen hurrying. His fight has three
	phases: the court (brimstone and a curse), the crown (fire, never under his own aura, and
	lashes of cinders), the cooling crown (chaos, and a guard). Most who fought him failed; a few
	spoke with him and regret it. He greets the player as "Midworld's little champion."

## The daemons

Daemons come through the cracks too, alone, and are the strongest things of their kind. The
Dark and the Black Daemon shrug off the hurt spells (immune to arcane); the Black one does not
sleep.

- **Grey Daemon** (`grey_daemon`, 46). Hunted so long its skin faded to old stone. Slow but never
	stops, and passes a barred door like a curtain. Sages say it was once a man who made a bargain
	with the wrong ledger; they do not say which ledger or which man. Resists earth strongly;
	weak to wind.
- **Red Daemon** (`red_daemon`, 48). A fire daemon that rides the lava veins under the volcanic
	hills past Endworld; its heat bends the air. Quick-tempered and proud; despises any foe who
	will not stand in front of it. Brimstone first, then cinders (it absorbs fire). Rain or snow
	enrages it ("Cool? I am NOT cool"). Wise travellers bring a cold drink and a wet cloak.
- **Dark Daemon** (`dark_daemon`, 49). One of the strongest to come through; hides in the shadows
	of the deepest ruins. Its hide turns aside blades that would pierce a dragon. It absorbs
	shadow and is immune to withering. Scholars say it serves the Demon King and cannot act
	without his leave. "Nobody has yet asked it."
- **Black Daemon** (`black_daemon`, 50). The most feared; mothers name it to frighten children
	on the road to Endworld. Its hide is harder than any armor Gilead's smiths have forged. A
	charged blow every 4th turn, a drain under half life, a guard under a quarter. Several knights
	tried to bind it; none came back to say how it went.

## Lucifuge

- **Lucifuge** (`lucifuge`, 50). The fallen seraph who rules the deepest pits beneath the realm.
	The sages of Endworld leave one warning: do not answer when his name is called. 600 hit
	points and a blow of 600. Immune to hurt and sleep magic: only blades and stubborn feet bring
	him down. He is courteous, which is worse. First he offers terms (a weakening) and tends his
	wounds; under two thirds the mask slips (a charged blow every 4th turn, every 3rd under a
	third); under a third he digs in.

## The tempters

Both belong to inns, never to the open road: the dark comes warm and asks you to sit.

- **Incubus** (`incubus`, 44). A tempter who appears as a handsome stranger at inns along the
	road to Narcissa and feeds on tired travellers who lower their guard for an hour's company.
	Priests in Hambry say a blessed cloth over the eyes breaks its illusion. Dreams you on its
	2nd, 6th turn; feeds on the sleeper or when it is hurt. Bring steel: it shrugs off the hurts.
- **Succubus** (`succubus`, 47). The Incubus's counterpart, in the same taverns and moonlit inns
	between Narcissa and Hambry. Trades warm words for a sip of a traveller's life and leaves the
	sleepers pale and calm. Saps your strength ("your sword is heavy"), drains you, and when losing
	lulls you.

## What they leave

Demon's Fall, Sin, Hate and Lie are demon trophies (`things/legendary-sets.md`). The Seraph's
set is dropped by seraphs, not by serafiends.
