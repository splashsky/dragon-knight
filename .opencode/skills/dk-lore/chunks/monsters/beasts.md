# Beasts

Hunters, herders and carters tell of them; farmers set traps. Most keep to their own ground and
seldom follow a traveller far, but a wounded or hungry one will. Where a beast stands over a
hive or a tree, it knows what to do with it: bears rake down a hive that hangs over you
(`things/battlefield-objects.md`).

## Small beasts and birds

- **Critter** (`critter`, 1). A scruffy little beast nesting in the hedgerows along the road out
	of Midworld. More nuisance than threat, though its claws leave scratches that take a week to
	heal. Farmers set traps; a pelt sells for a few coins at the Midworld market.
- **Creature** (`creature`, 2). A shaggy four-legged thing in the scrub past the Midworld road.
	Nobody has a proper name for it: the name came from a guard's report that left the box blank.
	Slow to anger, hits hard once roused.
- **Raven** (`raven`, 4). A large black bird on the dead trees along the road beyond Midworld,
	always watching. Steals anything shiny, including pack buckles. Hunters say a raven circling
	overhead is waiting for someone to fall.
- **Shadow Raven** (`shadow_raven`, 6). A raven dyed in shadow, circling the ruined towers near
	Bris after dusk. Spells cannot hurt it, so rely on steel. Scholars say it feeds on lost light.
- **Frost Raven** (`frost_raven`, 7). From the cold uplands north of Narcissa, feathers white with
	frost; strikes from above, and its chill lingers in the bone. It frosts you while you are
	awake (may put you to sleep). Hunters wear fur hats and call it the one bird they respect.
- **Fire Raven** (`fire_raven`, 9). A raven that sat too long by a forge, feather tips smouldering,
	in the scorched hills. It steals anything that glitters, including sparks off a smith's anvil,
	and sets you alight. Keep torches low and wait for it to fly off.

## Scorpions

Big as dogs. Poison is their sting, and they cannot be poisoned. All but the silver one are weak to fire.

- **Scorpion** (`scorpion`, 5). Desert-bred, the colour of old tea, under rocks along the road south
	of Roma. The tail is the danger: a hot ache that lasts a day. Caravan drivers shake out their
	boots every morning.
- **Rogue Scorpion** (`rogue_scorpion`, 7). Left the desert for thieving in the caravan camps south
	of Roma; darts in to take a purse. Roma merchants keep their coin in locked chests and pay the
	guards to watch the stables.
- **Dark Scorpion** (`dark_scorpion`, 15). As black as burnt wood, under stones in the dry hills
	past Narcissa. Its venom numbs an arm for a day; it opens with it and renews it every third
	turn. Immune to the hurt spells. Miners give it a wide berth and learn that only once.
- **Silver Scorpion** (`silver_scorpion`, 40). A shell like polished tin under the dunes beyond
	Hambry: 30 hit points but armor 350 and a sting of 160. Most blades turn. Dealers in Endworld
	pay well for an intact shell, one of the few things anyone has ever wanted from it.

## Wolves and the lycans

Lycans fear silver, in the old tongue: they are weak to holy light, and smiths charge double
for silver. They rage at night (the full moon).

- **Grey Wolf** (`grey_wolf`, 13). Gone grey with age: slower than the pack, craftier. Follows the
	caravans out of Bris and waits for a tired horse or a lost child. Herders keep dogs against
	them, and the dogs are usually first to go.
- **Dark Wolf** (`dark_wolf`, 17). Too big for its pack and too clever for hunters; roams the forest
	road past Kalle and hunts at dusk. Its coat turns plain blades and it shrugs off the hurt
	spells. It marks you on its first turn. The pack leader is the one that stays silent until it bites.
- **Werewolf** (`werewolf`, 17). A man bitten who has not yet stopped being a man. Waits at the
	edge of villages on full-moon nights; farmers around Kalle bar their doors and keep the dogs
	in. It still understands speech and mocks the hunters. Rages at night or under half; knits
	itself. Silver is the only thing that reliably ends one.
- **Lycan** (`lycan`, 25). A man who let the wolf in and never let it out. Hunts the roads between
	Narcissa and Hambry on nights of the full moon; a traveller who sees one rarely lives to say
	whose face it had.
- **Lycanthor** (`lycanthor`, 28). A lycan that kept its wits. Packs in the hill country past Kalle,
	sets traps, waits downwind of a camp. Marks you; survivors say it talks to its pack in a low
	voice that sounds like their own.
- **Lycanthra** (`lycanthra`, 30). The female kin and the pack leader in every pack she joins;
	quieter, harder to track. Takes men's shapes to draw travellers off the road near Endworld;
	her eyes stay the same colour in both forms. Marks, venoms, calls the moon below two
	thirds, heals under a third.

## Bears

Slow to anger and terrible once roused. The Grey Bear's pelt sells well in Gilead; furriers there
pay for the Dark Bear's.

- **Grey Bear** (`grey_bear`, 21). The common bear of the northern forests past Hambry, grizzled.
	Will sit on a road for a day rather than leave its cub's stream, so travellers go round by the ford.
- **Dark Bear** (`dark_bear`, 24). The black-furred cousin in the deep woods past Gilead where the
	trees grow too close for light. Shrugs off most hurt spells (leave it to the swordsmen). Keeps
	to its own hollows and guards a den a long time.
- **Werebear** (`werebear`, 26). A hunter who went into the forest one winter and came out heavier.
	Keeps to the wooded slopes between Kalle and Narcissa, most dangerous in spring when it is
	hungry and the honey is not yet in. Hunters who meet one leave their bows on the ground and run.

A bear also answers the player's call: Call Bear (`magic/allies.md`). In the mountains above
Endworld a bear that has taken a chieftain's life is skinned and its claws go to the clan's
grave-mound; the claws that fall from living bears are strung as the Bearclaw Totem.

## Great hunters

- **Beast** (`beast`, 23). A shapeless hunter of the far hills near Gilead, fur and claw and teeth
	with no clear face. Scholars argue whether it is one creature or a kind. It hunts alone, hides in
	snow, and follows a wounded traveller for days. Carry more bandages than seems sensible.
- **Large Beast** (`large_beast`, 27). Grey, shaggy, higher than a cart horse at the shoulder; comes
	down from the high ground around Hambry in hard winters following the deer. Hunters cut a notch in
	their bows for each one seen and not met.
- **Behemoth** (`behemoth`, 48). Of the old marshes, barn-sized and armored like a turtle; eats whole
	trees and whatever sleeps beneath them. Its path is a wide flattened road in the reed-beds that
	runs from one ruined village to the next. Hungry not for men but for whatever is nearest. Upper
	echelon (`legends/upper-echelon.md`).

## The hell-kin

Fire in the coat, fire in the bite: resist fire, weak to frost, water and holy light.

- **Hellcat** (`hellcat`, 18). A cat the size of a large dog, fur singed black at the tips, eyes like
	coals. Waits on a ledge in the rocky hills between Roma and Bris and drops on lone riders; leaves
	the bones in neat piles, which rangers read as a warning. Nobody has made one purr.
- **Hellhound** (`hellhound`, 20). Runs in pairs and packs along the hot road between Bris and
	Kalle; its sulphur breath is smelt a mile off, the only warning. Faster than any horse, so fight
	from a doorway. Sets you alight once. Old soldiers in Kalle keep a hound's tooth as a charm.
- **Hellbeast** (`hellbeast`, 22). Bred long in the fire pits under Hambry, bigger than either, with
	horns and a hide that shrugs off the first blow. The cult that runs the pits keeps them as
	guards and sometimes loosens them on the roads. Immune to fire. Heats the field and rages in the heat.

## The walking plant

- **Nightshade** (`nightshade`, 6). A poisonous plant that walks on its roots, in shady glades and
	forest edges near Kalle. The sap itches, the bite is worse; it drinks poison (absorbs it) and
	burns well. Herbalists burn the plant on purpose to keep it from spreading and wear gloves.
