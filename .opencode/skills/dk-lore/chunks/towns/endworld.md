# Endworld

## Where it is

At -250, -250: the south-west corner of the map, on the edge itself, 250 steps from Midworld on the south-west arm
(`world/overview.md`). Its land is the Ashlands (`lands/ashlands.md`): grey ash that puffs at every step, black
volcanic glass in shards, cooling cinders that tick, red glow in the cracks, burnt trunks, dead hills steep and
black. Smoke rises from vents. Heat rolls in from the edge of the world in the afternoon.

## What it is known for

- **Being last.** "Endworld" is the end of the map and of the road order; behind it there is only the
	edge (`world/overview.md`). Its inn costs 125, the most.
- **The legendary shop.** It sells the Destiny Blade, Raiment and Aegis (`things/legendary-sets.md`), the
	Starsteel Sword, the Storm Lance and the Dragonhide Shield, and little else.
- **Wardens who do not sleep.** "A sleeping man out there is a dead one." The wardens wear the Sentinel Charm on
	the chest and not in a pocket, and the gate wardens are said to cast Barrier on each other in turns.
- **Ash and heat.** People tie a wet cloth over the mouth before a fight in cinders. Scouts carry wet sacks "as a
	matter of habit". They say a man who fears fire should stay out of the ash, and that the ash will find him anyway.

## Who lives here

- **Wardens and the garrison**: the garrison's pay once stopped for four months, and a sergeant took his
	squad over the wall: the Renegade, who tolls the road he used to guard. Guards say a Halo Charm "will not stop a
	claw, but it does make a man stand up straighter when the claw comes".
- **Merchants**: sell Starsteel and "will tell you the price is the cost of the ore, which is true and not the
	whole story". Mercenaries buy the Void Flask here by weight. Dealers pay well for a Silver Scorpion's intact shell.
- **Storm-watchers**: string a copper wire down each lance and hang it from a tower in the spring; "three or four
	good storms and the wire holds enough charge to be worth carrying".
- **Mages**: fire the Lightning Lance "down the long road at anything that walks up it, then sit on the stones for a
	turn while their heads stop ringing". The old masters say Inferno "was meant for clearing roads, and roads are
	what it clears". Renewal "leaves the caster more tired than the fight did".
- **Priests, sages, hunters**: priests sell Dragon's Tear "at a high price"; sages leave a single warning about
	Lucifuge; hunters say a Rend cast well makes a beast forget where it was going.
- **Masons and cutters**: masons pile rubble on steam cracks they can reach; glass cutters' pits are dug into the
	ground, and obsidian is knapped into blades that keep an edge.
- **People of the plain**: the starsteel's iron "comes from a meteor that fell on the Endworld plain before anyone
	lived there". Past the corner, clan women keep the claws of chieftain-killing bears, and shepherds carry their
	lamps through the winter.

## The market

Inn: 125 gold, the dearest. It sells the map of Gilead only. Endworld's own map costs 9,000 gold; travel there
costs 160 Travel Points, the most.

- **Weapons**: Starsteel Sword (25,000), Storm Lance (35,000), Destiny Blade (50,000).
- **Armor**: Destiny Raiment (50,000). **Shields**: Dragonhide Shield (20,000), Destiny Aegis (25,000).
- **Accessories**: Halo Charm, Sentinel Charm.
- **Consumables**: Herb, Potion, Ether, Cure-All, Elixir, Void Flask, Fire Ward Draught.

The weapons are the dearest sold anywhere; the Destiny Aegis is "rarely for sale, and buyers have to be known to
the smiths". The Dragonhide Shield is "rarely made", for few hunters can bring a dragon back whole.

## The land around it

The Ashlands (`lands/ashlands.md`): ash and cinders, steam vents, lava vents, volcanic hills, obsidian. Beneath it
are cellars and sewers (where a demon climbed out, and silver slimes live) and fire-lit halls where seraphs
drop their gifts. At a crossroads near it the gallows once stood. A road runs in from Gilead. Past the town, off the map:
a sea-coast, a deep lake, mountains and high valleys, and a stronghold of demons (`world/overview.md`).

## Monsters

At the gate, levels 49 to 50: Colossus, Dark Daemon, Demon King, Black Daemon, Lucifuge, Siren, Titan. These
are the top of the world (`legends/upper-echelon.md`). By lore, Endworld's own:

- **On the plain and the road**: Fire in the ash fields (24); Fear on the road (23); Terror at the old crossroads
	(31); Lycanthra in men's shapes (30); a Fire Wyrm in the lava vents (32); a Silver Slime in the old sewers (30).
- **Soldiers**: the Renegade (42); the Greatest Devil, whose name is painted red on the gates (43); Red Dragon
	asleep beneath the mountains (41); Red Daemon in the volcanic hills (48).
- **Beyond the corner**: the Leviathan in the lake (46), the Siren on the rocks of the sea-coast (50), the Titan in
	the mountains (50), the Colossus at the pass (49), and the demon host: Demon Prince (47), Demon Lord (48), Demon
	King (49), Black Daemon (50) and Lucifuge (50) (`monsters/demons.md`).

## Rumours and habits

- The Greatest Devil's name is "painted on the gates of Endworld in red by people who did not want to be the next
	to hear it".
- Of Lucifuge: "do not answer when his name is called". Of the Black Daemon: "the one mothers name to frighten
	children on the road to Endworld".
- Seraph's Love and Seraph's Truth are dropped "in the fire-lit halls" and "deepest halls" under the town; Demon's
	Hate came from "a demon that climbed out of the cellars of Endworld".
- A steam vent's hiss "comes a turn before the scald", which the people learn to hear.
