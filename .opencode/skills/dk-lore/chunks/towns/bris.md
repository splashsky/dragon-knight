# Bris

## Where it is

At 70, -70: north-west of Midworld, on the line that carries the storms to Gilead, and the
same line that Kalle lies on the other side of (`world/overview.md`). Its land is the Salt
Shoals (`lands/salt-shoals.md`): grey tidal flats, shell banks, shallow channels, salt marsh
and weed. Hills and cliffs stand behind it; ruined towers and mines lie to the east.

## What it is known for

- **Pearls and divers.** Divers work the tidal shoals south of the town with a rope and a knife,
	and wade out to the shell beds every day: "a diver who is afraid of the water is the first to
	drown." The shallow beds are picked clean every spring. Mages buy the pearls by the handful and
	grind them for ink.
- **Apothecaries and smiths.** It has "the big apothecary" and a chemist, and the smiths, tinsmiths
	and silversmiths of a trading town (below).
- **Rain, sea mist and the storms' road**, and the carters' saying that a wet road is worse than a
	long one (`world/weather-and-time.md`).
- **Where the wild begins.** The caravans and nomads go on from Bris toward Kalle across the dry
	country, and the Hellhounds and Wyverns hunt that road.

## Who lives here

- **Pearl divers and fishermen**: they cut swamp-tortoise shell into squares for harness; the
	fishwives say a broken tide pool is the cheapest cure for a burning cloak.
- **The big apothecary**: sells Hi-Potions to caravan guards on contract, a dozen at a time, and
	has them sign for each bottle. Other apothecaries keep venom in a locked cabinet and sell it by
	the drop.
- **A chemist** who sells Acid Vials, wears gloves to the market and will not say where the acid
	comes from. Sailors buy it for barnacles, "and a few of them use it on other things."
- **Smiths and tinsmiths**: spiked targes made to order; rime rings sold by weight; spark rings
	for anyone who asks about the storms; a silversmith who makes traveller's rings in a day.
- **Innkeepers**: pay for Dream when a guest will not settle, buy wakeful rings for the night
	watch, and have sold travel tea since one told a customer it was "the only thing he had ever sold that people came
	back for".
- **Priests and Paladins**: the Paladins of Bris call Consecrate "the ground's own blessing" and
	learn Radiance at fifty-eight. Priests there teach Dawnstrike with the warning that "a wounded man
	should not wait for the dawn to come to him", teach Life for free, call Tithe "the cheapest mana in
	the realm", and sell a blessing that makes spirits easier to hit. See `magic/paladins.md`.
- **Beekeepers east of the town**: sell marsh-hornet stings for a penny each, as warnings to thieves.
- **Carters, caravan guards, hunters**: a hunting party out of Bris brought down the guardian
	that left Angel's Love; the guards pay a bounty for drakelor claws. See `peoples/roads-and-outlaws.md`.

## The market

Inn: 25 gold. It sells the maps of Roma and Kalle. Bris's own map costs 50 gold; travel here costs
15 Travel Points.

- **Weapons**: Branch, Club, Dagger, Hatchet, Sickle.
- **Armor**: Clothes, Leather Armor, Hard Leather Armor, Apprentice Robe, Scout's Jerkin.
	**Shields**: Buckler, Parry Buckler, Small Shield, Spiked Targe.
- **Accessories**: Ember Ring, Rime Ring, Spark Ring, Serpent Ring, Traveller's Ring, Wakeful Ring,
	Lucky Charm.
- **Consumables**: Herb, Antidote, Potion, Hi-Potion, Regen Potion, Ether, Fire Flask, Sleep Powder,
	Acid Vial, Fire Ward Draught, Frost Ward Draught, Trail Snack, Travel Tea.

New here: the first resistance draughts, the Hi-Potion and the Regen Potion, and rings that
resist frost and lightning. There is no Storm Ward Draught: it begins at Kalle (`towns/kalle.md`).

## The land around it

The Salt Shoals (`lands/salt-shoals.md`): flats, channels and shell banks, with ponds and lakes
(`lands/waters-and-heights.md`). Cliffs and hills above the town hold caves and young dragons;
there are ruined towers, old mine galleries to the east, boulders on the roads (life stones are
found under them, and farmers set them beside the sick). To the west and the north-east lies the dry country
the nomads cross (`lands/dry-country.md`).

## Monsters

At the gate, levels 13 to 15: Grey Wolf, Megaskeleton, Phantom, Specter, Dark Scorpion, Orc,
Sylph, Warlock. By lore:

- **Drakes of the cliffs and caves**: Drakemal in the hills (6), a Drakelor on the road to Roma
	(3), young dragons on the cliffs (the Dragon's Claw's first owner, `legends/upper-echelon.md`),
	and the Wyverns of the cliffs over the road to Kalle (33, 32 for the lesser).
- **Grey Wolves** follow the caravans out of Bris; Hellhounds run the hot road toward Kalle (20).
- **Shadow Ravens** circle the ruined towers after dusk (6); a **Terra Elemental** in the galleries
	east (25); a **Flame** is why the smiths keep a barrel of water at the door (28).
- **Sorcerors** are "common in the towns past Bris, where the libraries are cheap" (11).
- Beasts and elementals: `monsters/beasts.md`, `monsters/drakes-and-dragons.md`.

## Rumours and habits

- "Bris has a saying about men who fight angry and lose their heads" (the Damage Up fury).
- Bris sellers say a warm hand is a lucky one. Ember rings turn up in old camps along the road,
	left by those who did not come back.
- Bris healers say a Serpent Ring will not stop a bite, "it only cuts down the poison that comes after".
