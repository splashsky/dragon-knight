# Narcissa

## Where it is

At -130, -130: south-west of Midworld, on the south-west arm, 130 steps out (`world/overview.md`).
Its land is the Greymoor (`lands/greymoor.md`): grey-purple heather cut by black peaty water, standing
stones, cairns, the rusted ribs of siege engines half sunk in the heather. The town is walled, with
bronze bells on the walls, and the smoke of its kilns and foundries can be seen far across the moor.

## What it is known for

- **Fog.** It comes off the low ground at dawn, about one dawn in two, and stays until the sun is well
	up (`world/weather-and-time.md`). "The best time of the year for business", say the bandits, and the
	carters wait at the crossroads for it to lift while the innkeepers sell cheap ale.
- **Fire and clay.** Foundries whose furnaces are never let go out, potters' kilns that also fire the
	roof tiles, and fire-mages who put a cup of lamp oil on the head of every staff they sell.
- **Glass**: cutters make the frost charm's pearl and, after dark, the shade charm's black slab.
- **The college and its stills.** The mages' college makes Hi-Ether in copper stills on its north wall
	("the smell of it set off too many fires in the old lodgings"), labels each flask with its maker, and
	will not sell to anyone it does not know.
- **A watch that shuts the gates.** When the light goes "that particular grey", the watchmen close up
	(`magic/mages.md`: Nightfall).

## Who lives here

- **Foundry men and potters**: forge the Cinder Blade (quenched in brine, "warm for a week") and bake
	the Cinder Shield; mercenaries bound for the cold country buy blades by the pair.
- **Fire-mages**: the oil cup on the staff "does nothing for the spell", but "a student who can see the
	flame feels braver". Most learn to leave it off by their second winter. Mages of Narcissa learned
	Regen from watching the town's cats; they do not argue that Sap Mind is the thief's spell; and say Sunfire
	is "the best reason to be a Mage in summer". They swear an arcane bolt will tip a standing stone.
- **Guards**: drink Guard Tonic before a night watch, and say the copper of a spark ring "draws a bolt off
	before it finds the hand". The bronze of the bells on the walls is the bronze of the Ward Charm discs.
- **Judges**: keep a box of Angel's Truth lenses for disputes; "they cannot lie without the lens
	clouding first".
- **Healers**: pour the Bitter Draught from a wide clay jug in the old infirmary; most drink it once,
	and carry it all their lives.
- **Armourers**: sell warhammers "to men who have already decided to take the hits".
- **Apothecaries**: sell Cure-All and no Antidote (the Viper Charm's Antidote is Kalle's affair: `towns/kalle.md`).
- **Priests**: say a spirit is what is left of someone who has not finished their business.
- **Dreamers**: say a figment fades if you stop believing in it.
- **Bell-ringers** in the towns near Narcissa sound the alarm when a demon is seen on the road.
- **Bandits and carters**: see Fog. See `peoples/roads-and-outlaws.md`.

## The market

Inn: 60 gold. It sells the maps of Kalle and Hambry. Narcissa's own map costs 500 gold; travel there
costs 50 Travel Points.

- **Weapons**: Dagger, Brand, Broadsword, Claymore, Cinder Blade, Dark Sword, Ember Staff, Warhammer.
- **Armor**: Chain Mail, Lamellar Armor, Bronze Plate, Iron Plate, Ironclad Plate, Rimefur Coat.
	**Shields**: Buckler, Small Shield, Large Shield, Cinder Shield.
- **Accessories**: Lucky Charm, Spark Ring, Wakeful Ring, Flame, Frost, Shade, Storm, Viper and Ward Charms.
- **Consumables**: Herb, Potion, Hi-Potion, Ether, Hi-Ether, Cure-All, Bitter Draught, Guard Tonic,
	Strength Tonic, Weakening Dust, Acid Vial, Fire Flask, Explosive Barrel, Fire, Frost and Storm Ward
	Draughts.

New here: the first swords, the cinder gear, Hi-Ether, Cure-All, the Shade Charm and the
Ward Charm. The market has no Antidote: the first town without one.

## The land around it

The Greymoor (`lands/greymoor.md`): heather, peat, standing stones and old battlefields grassed over,
with river gravel that yields topaz and scorched ground left by fire spells cast badly and left burning.
Past it lie dry hills, valleys held by orcs, caves and old mines, a pass where carts come up a grade,
ruined towers, cold uplands to the north with passes above, marshes beyond (`lands/thornbog.md`), and
the dead school (`monsters/undead.md`). Wooded slopes lead back toward Kalle, and roads toward Hambry.

## Monsters

At the gate, levels 25 to 27: Fright, Lycan, Polgergeist, Terra Elemental, Dawkor, Necromancer,
Ultrawraith, Werebear, Brute, Horror, Large Beast. By lore, in Narcissa's country:

- **Orcs and their kin** in the hills past it: Orcs and Uruks, with Dawks, Dawkor and Brutes holding the
	caves, the mines and the pass (15 to 27). Caravans pay a toll to the orcs, then another on the way back.
- **Cold things**: a Frost Raven on the northern uplands (7), the Frost Wyrm in the passes (30) and ice
	wolves higher still.
- **Old dead**: Ghouls on the old battlefields (7); the dead school beyond, with its Liche (35), Liche
	Master (38) and Liche Prince (40), and its necromancers who teach with Miasma.
- **At night**: a Nightmare in the inns beyond (33), an Incubus at the inns on the road in (44), a
	Succubus on the roads toward Hambry (47), the Shadow Dragon on the empty roads after dusk (44).
- **Scorched ground** holds a Fira Elemental (34), and a Black Dragon is said to have hunted the marshes
	beyond "since before the towns were walled" (47).
- Wizards (20) camp in ruined towers past it. See `legends/upper-echelon.md` for the dragons.

## Rumours and habits

- A hunting party out of Narcissa brought home a Dragon's Tooth; "that did not all come home".
- Mages keep a charm of salt by the bed against Nightmare.
- Hunters wear fur hats on the northern uplands, "and say the frost raven is the one bird they respect".
- Miners leave a lamp burning at the cave mouth, because a Dawk will wait a whole day for someone to
	come within reach.
- Bells from distant Narcissa drift over the heather.
