# Gilead

## Where it is

At 200, -200: north-west of Midworld, 200 steps out along the line that Bris is on, and the place where the
storms end (`world/weather-and-time.md`). Its land is the Storm Barrens (`lands/storm-barrens.md`): wind that
never stops and "smells of rain a mile away", broken walls, fallen gates, old bones and rust where battles ended,
barred crypts, flooded mine workings, salt flats. The walls of Gilead stand on the skyline.

## What it is known for

- **Where the storms end.** Every storm formed near Midworld is carried here and breaks over the walls.
	Its wardens carry storm ward draught on every watch and buy storm charms "before every season of
	high winds".
- **Blizzards.** "Gilead gets the worst of them."
- **Walls and a garrison.** An old garrison, with knights, standards and tower shields that "hold a
	gate"; a Strong Knight there still holds the gate that he was told he was not strong enough to hold.
- **Glass and secret alloys**: clear unannealed glass blades, black Void Flasks from sand whose name no one will say, and a sunsteel mail that sells "perhaps one a year".
- **The temple and its smiths**, and the thornwood priests of the bog.

## Who lives here

- **The garrison and its captains**: buy sentinel charms by the dozen; "a standard comes down at the first
	good blow", and a knight who has lost his banner "fights like a man who has lost his temper". Guard captains
	warn recruits to watch the rocks above them in the passes. Caravan guards out of Gilead know the brutes of
	the road.
- **Temple smiths and priests**: forge the Dawn Mace from the silver of a sunken bell, with a small sun cut
	into the flange. Priests bless the Vigil Cuirass ("each charge is one night of watch") and say Iron Vigil
	is faith, and "the mercenaries who drink with them say it is the prayer and the cold". The priests will
	not say who taught them Curse; they keep a cure ready for anyone named in the Doom book. Knights call
	Judgement "the honest spell".
- **Glassmiths and smiths**: sell the Glass Blade "with a warning on the box"; keep the Void Flask receipts
	in a locked box, "because they have had one blow up in a shop". The smiths of Gilead will not name the
	alloy of the sunsteel mail, and pay well for diamonds.
- **A family of alchemists**: one workshop makes Elixir, "the only remedy the border towns trust"; the motto
	is painted over the door, "and the price is painted over the motto".
- **Horse traders**: sell War Sabres with the saddles, "and the sabre usually costs more than the horse".
- **Bog priests**: carve thornwood hearts into charms and say the wood beats at the full moon; the folk
	say the first thornwood root dug up was a hand. The Mages' guild buys every root out of the bog.
- **Furriers**: pay well for the hides of Dark Bears and Grey Bears.
- **Scholars**: say the Archfiend was bound in the north and walked out when the seal cracked; that the
	Ultradoomer's count is the only real thing about it; and that Nightmare was written by a Mage who could not
	sleep. "Old Gilead has a saying that the Mage who lives on other people's blood is still a Mage, but a thin
	one" (Withering).
- **Crowds in the squares**: the Demagogue "gathers crowds in the squares of Gilead" and tells them the
	crown is starving them on purpose. In the deep towns, Chaos "has ended more than one lord's bodyguard".

## The market

Inn: 100 gold. It sells the maps of Hambry and Endworld. Gilead's own map costs 3,000 gold; travel there
costs 110 Travel Points.

- **Weapons**: Dark Axe, Dark Sword, Bright Sword, Blessed Mace, Dawn Mace, War Sabre, Glass Blade,
	Magic Sword.
- **Armor**: Dark Armor, Bright Armor, Magic Armor, Ogre-hide Cuirass, Vigil Cuirass, Sunsteel Mail.
	**Shields**: Lifeward Shield, Ward Shield, Silver Shield, Tower Shield.
- **Accessories**: Halo, Sentinel, Shade and Storm Charms.
- **Consumables**: Herb, Potion, Ether, Hi-Ether, Cure-All, Bitter Draught, Guard Tonic, Elixir, Void
	Flask, Explosive Barrel, Fire Ward and Storm Ward Draughts.

New here: Dark and Bright armour, the Magic Sword and Glass Blade, the Dawn Mace, Sunsteel Mail, the
Silver and Tower Shields, Elixir and Void Flask. The last town, Endworld, sells only the legendary
(`towns/endworld.md`).

## The land around it

The Storm Barrens (`lands/storm-barrens.md`): old battlefields, broken towers, crypts and chapels half hidden
in the grass, flooded mines, salt flats, and a thornwood bog (`lands/thornbog.md`). Past it: ruins where a
demon fed, crypts and graveyards, battlefields where vultures "followed the armies for a generation", high
passes (seraphs die there; Dark Knights hold the roads), hills east for an ogre, far hills for the Beast,
deep woods where the trees "grow too close for light", high valleys where snow lasts into summer, marshes
beyond, and a crossroads south where something counts. The road to Endworld runs from here.

## Monsters

At the gate, levels 39 to 41: Blue Dragon, Pale Knight, Devil, Liche Prince, Obsessive, Silver Scorpion,
Cherufiend, Greater Devil, Red Dragon. At Gilead's gate a fighter meets the dragons.

By lore, Gilead's own:

- **Near**: Shimmers over the salt flats in the hottest weeks (8), a Magician thrown out of a tower near
	Gilead (8), Silver Slimes in cellars (30), Aqua Elementals in the flooded mines (31), a Strong Knight at
	the gate (35), Greatest Wyverns on the ridge road (37).
- **Beyond**: Ultraskeletons and Necromancers in the crypts and graveyards (16, 26), Megawraiths on the
	battlefields (21), a Beast, an Ogre, a Dark Bear (23, 23, 24), Horrors and Megadoomers at the roads (27, 34),
	Dark Knights in the passes (43), Giants in the valleys (43), Nihilist and Demagogue among the learned (47,
	48), and the Knight on the dusk road to Endworld (30). See `monsters/undead.md`, `monsters/giants.md`,
	`legends/upper-echelon.md`.
- Demons that fell from the walls and fed in the ruins are the source of Demon's Fall and Demon's Sin.

## Rumours and habits

- The dead of Gilead "are said to have stopped reaching the afterlife" because of the Megawraiths.
- Seraph's Joy "falls from a seraph's wing when the creature is slain in the high passes beyond Gilead".
- The Void Flask is blown "from sand that the miners bring up from below the salt marsh and no one will say
	the name of".
- Salt traders say the Shimmer appears only in the hottest weeks, "and that anyone who follows it is not seen
	again".
- Every soldier who has served on the Barrens has seen a standard come down in a squall, "and nobody talks
	about what happened next."
