# Hambry

## Where it is

At 170, 170: north-east of Midworld, on the same line as Roma, 170 steps out (`world/overview.md`). Its
land is the Hambry Weald (`lands/hambry-weald.md`): close grey-green forest, moss on every trunk,
river marshes, deep pools, high pastures and bare windy ridges, and an underworld of workings and
pits. It lies north of latitude 100, so what falls there is snow.

## What it is known for

- **Snow.** "Snow falls on Hambry and Gilead for half the year, and the people there have stopped
	complaining about it." Ice forms on the north roads. On bad nights the guards ring a bell at the
	gate "so the lost can find the road" (`world/weather-and-time.md`).
- **Faith and healing.** An abbey makes Cure-All; a temple blesses maces; surgeons and healers work
	the road's wounds.
- **The watch.** Its watch guild gives every guildsman a Sentinel Charm on the night he first stands
	a gate. Its guards are paid, and wear the city's greatswords.
- **The hall of the pleaders.** The Advocate, the Fanatic, the Zealot and the Obsessive hold a hall
	where "anyone who comes in without a receipt" is judged (`monsters/people.md`).
- **Mages of the high spells.** Hambry mages know Rockfall, Hail, Cyclone and Starfall, which "cannot
	be bought in a shop".

## Who lives here

- **The abbey**: its cellars make Cure-All, "a dark green syrup" first made for monks who came back
	from the marsh with blood poisoning; it costs more than most earn in a week, "because it works on
	everything". Gaia's priests will not teach Plague.
- **The temple**: a priest has blessed more maces than he can count; pilgrims going north buy
	them; the temple and Gilead's sell the Halo Charm, "keep the price high so that the faithful
	value them". The priests keep a whole book on haunts, "most of it grim", and teach that a blessed
	cloth over the eyes breaks an incubus's illusion.
- **Healers and surgeons**: say a Lifeward Shield makes a wounded man bleed slower; healers say Mercy
	is wasted on a scratch; they sell frost ward draught on the road.
- **Guards and watchmen**: guards at the gate learn Fend "before they learn to swing". Mages buy
	Ward Shields for their guards on the road, and the guards buy them for the free charges.
- **Mages**: swear by Barrier before a long march; "the Mages of Hambry have made it harder" to live on
	soft earth (Rockfall); herbalists keep the Poison bottles the guard would rather not inventory;
	hedge-wizards grind Magic Rock into powder; wind-readers claim Cyclone can be seen from the Weald;
	Hambry men say Hail is the quickest way to clear a crowded road. See `magic/mages.md`.
- **Hunters, trappers, honey-cutters**: hunters of the Weald keep a tree between themselves and open
	ground; trappers say a Frost Charm is cold for a week after it is bought; honey-cutters walk under
	the skeps each morning with a smoking pot; hunters in the hills trade ogre-hide cuirasses as trophies.
- **Fishers and shepherds**: fishers near the river say the first they know of a Deluge is the wet, and
	that a drakefin will take a line; shepherds on the high pastures count their flocks twice a day since
	the wyverns came.
- **Minstrels, a monk and a trader**: minstrels play for their supper in the inns (the Bard's Pipe); a monk
	above the town grinds Sage's Lenses at cost; a trader sells Hawk Eyes and says the bird was his.
- **Miners**: dig the emerald from the deep workings past the town, say a fright follows the sound of
	their picks down into the dark, and fear the Dawkin gangs.

## The market

Inn: 90 gold. It sells the maps of Narcissa and Gilead. Hambry's own map costs 1,000 gold; travel there
costs 80 Travel Points.

- **Weapons**: Battle Axe, Claymore, Greatsword, Warhammer, Dark Axe, Dark Sword, Cinder Blade,
	War Sabre, Blessed Mace, Bright Sword.
- **Armor**: Iron Plate, Ironclad Plate, Ogre-hide Cuirass, Vigil Cuirass, Magic Armor.
	**Shields**: Small Shield, Large Shield, Cinder Shield, Lifeward Shield, Ward Shield.
- **Accessories**: Flame, Frost, Halo, Sentinel, Shade, Storm, Viper and Ward Charms.
- **Consumables**: Herb, Potion, Hi-Potion, Ether, Hi-Ether, Cure-All, Bitter Draught, Guard Tonic,
	Strength Tonic, Weakening Dust, Fire, Frost and Storm Ward Draughts.

New here: the Greatsword and War Sabre, the Blessed Mace and Bright Sword, the first blessed gear
(Vigil Cuirass, Lifeward and Ward Shields, Halo and Sentinel Charms) and Magic Armor. It sells no
Explosive Barrel and no food; the market is a soldier's.

## The land around it

The Hambry Weald (`lands/hambry-weald.md`): woods that the fighters know, river marshes where the fog
rises "the way steam comes off a kettle", deep pools, snowbound pastures and bare ridges where the wind
never stops, with a scorched road, ruined mills, churches and toll houses, a ruined inn or manor outside the
walls, and a dead hall beneath the road (`monsters/undead.md`). Beyond it: forests to the north,
mountain passes, a marsh-forest (`lands/thornbog.md`), dunes, and fire pits worked by a cult (`monsters/demons.md`).

## Monsters

At the gate, levels 33 to 35: Armor Knight, Nightmare, Wyvern, Fira Elemental, Greater Wyvern, Megadoomer,
Advocate, Liche, Strong Knight. By lore, Hambry's own:

- **The hall**: Advocate (35), Fanatic (36), Zealot (38, walking the Gilead road with a list of names),
	Obsessive (40) and the Traitor who sold the gate keys (45) (`monsters/people.md`).
- **Marsh and wood**: Drakefin (8), Aqua Elemental in the deep pools (31), Grey Bears in the north
	forests (21), the Green Dragon in the marsh-forest (36), a Haunt in a ruined inn (22).
- **Heights**: Sylphs in the passes (15), Aero Elementals on the ridges (29), Greater Wyverns on the pastures
	(34), a Large Beast down from the high ground in hard winters (27), Silver Scorpions in the dunes beyond (40).
- **Below**: Hellbeasts and Hellions from the fire pits (22, 16), Dawkin in the mines (21), Fright (25)
	in the old mills, Ultrawraiths in ruined churches (26), the Ultradoomer in the dead hall (36), and the
	Dark Dragon, asleep in the mountain under Hambry (45). See `legends/upper-echelon.md`.
- **Roads**: Rogues work the Hambry road (8); Lycans and the Succubus the roads toward Narcissa.

## Rumours and habits

- The Dark Dragon "collects names" and wakes about once in a generation.
- A haunt in the marsh-forest is said to call up the fog on purpose.
- Angel's Rise drifts "from the ground after a guardian is struck down in the hills past Hambry"; the wardens
	tie the feathers to their helms so they can find them on the march.
- Hambry's smiths make greatswords "for the city's paid guards, who need a blade that can open a crowd or a
	door with the same swing".
- Demon's Lie came from "a demon that spoke in a merchant's voice outside Hambry": "the best deal they
	ever struck, and then the worst."
