# Roma

## Where it is

At 30, 30: north-east of Midworld, thirty steps along the north-east arm, the nearest of the
seven. Its land is the Copper Downs (`lands/copper-downs.md`): wheat-coloured grass over hill
roads, chalk tracks, stone walls and sheep. A road runs on from Roma toward Bris in the
north-west (`world/overview.md`).

## What it is known for

- **Copper, glass and clocks.** Copper roofs go green on the downs, a glassworks smokes over the
	ridge, and a clock strikes somewhere below the road.
- **Rain and mud.** Rain is the common weather of Roma and Bris, and the farmers would not
	know what to do with a dry year. The mud stays after it. See `world/weather-and-time.md`.
- **The Mages' guild**, which sells robes to apprentices "will tell you so at length".
- **Goblins in the hills.** Every goblin camp on the Copper Downs has a campfire; every drovers'
	yard out of Roma has one too, and a bucket by the door.

## Who lives here

- **Guild masters and Mages**: they teach Cinder Lash ("cast it first and talk later") and keep
	Rain Call for long summer days. Most own a spark rod and keep it in the belt, where the
	neighbours can see it. A Mage in Roma once fired three Hyper Beams in a row and fell over,
	which is why the spell comes with a warning.
- **Copper workers, glassmen, clockmakers and jewellers**: spark rods, ember rings, wakeful rings
	("it was a clock spring before it was a ring, and they will tell you so before you have
	finished asking") and serpent rings, for the snake-catchers who sell venom to apothecaries.
- **Guards**: they have been known to take a dozen daggers from one traveller, and say the trick
	with Sleep Powder is to be upwind. Merchants pay them to watch the stables.
- **Paladins and priests**: "the sun rises for the one who is still standing". The priests
	bless silver shields for a fee. The innkeepers say a Paladin who can cast Mend needs fewer rooms.
- **Warriors and brawlers**: Warriors learn Mire at twenty-one in the training yards, "where the
	mud is kept fresh for the purpose". Tavern fighters use Craze before a brawl, and old hands swear
	the blood is the point of Blood Strike.
- **Ordinary people**: ironmongers with a bin of sickles by the door; duellists with parry
	bucklers; washerwomen who keep a bucket by the door and "count themselves lucky when it hisses";
	street children who say the mud is theirs by right; scholars who pay for memory drops;
	a tinker who stopped making buckles after the third return; hunters who swear Ember keeps a wolf
	from coming back; old women who know what a dybbuk is. See `peoples/trades.md`.

## The market

Inn: 10 gold. It sells the maps of Midworld and Bris. Roma's own map costs 25 gold (at Midworld);
travel here costs 5 Travel Points.

- **Weapons**: Branch, Club, Dagger, Sickle, Spark Rod.
- **Armor**: Clothes, Leather Armor, Apprentice Robe, Scout's Jerkin. **Shields**: Buckler,
	Parry Buckler.
- **Accessories**: Copper Ring, Ember Ring, Serpent Ring, Traveller's Ring, Wakeful Ring.
- **Consumables**: Herb, Antidote, Potion, Ether, Fire Flask, Sleep Powder, Trail Snack, Travel Tea.

New here: the first potions and ether, the first rings that resist an element or a status,
and the Spark Rod for Mages. The market also charges for the antidote that Cleanse makes
unnecessary.

## The land around it

The Copper Downs (`lands/copper-downs.md`): hills and chalk tracks, goblin camps, old chapels on
the rises whose bells have stopped, cliffs and caves to the north, marsh mists past them, a dry
road south, and farms about the town. The rocky hills toward Bris are cat country. Gravel on the
hill roads yields magic stones; goblin camps hang life rocks round their necks. Bears come down
from the hills in autumn for honey, which is why Call Bear works (`magic/allies.md`).

## Monsters

At the gate, levels 5 to 7: Illusion, Scorpion, Drakemal, Ghost, Nightshade, Shadow Raven,
Frost Raven, Ghoul, Rogue Scorpion. By lore Roma's own are:

- **Goblins** in the hills along the road out (level 10; a Goblin Shaman, level 5, does not roam).
- **Scorpions** on the road south and Rogue Scorpions in the caravan camps (5, 7).
- **An Illusion** in the marsh mists (5), **a Dybbuk** (9; "a debt left unpaid to its family") and
	**a Spirit** in the ruins outside the town (18), **a Nisse** in the barns (19).
- **A Wyrm** in the caves under the cliffs north (28) and **Serafiends** in the old chapels above
	(38). Both are met far past Roma's ring; read them as the country beyond.
- **Drakes** on the road from Midworld, **Drakelors** and **Hellcats** toward Bris, a **Blaze** on
	the road to Bris (`monsters/drakes-and-dragons.md`, `monsters/others.md`,
	`monsters/fey-and-spirits.md`).

## Rumours and habits

- The heat of the afternoon, say the people, "is what Endworld breathes out"; Roma shuts its shutters through the
	hottest hours.
- A guardian that fell near Roma left a white feather, and the priests sell the one that is left over:
	Angel's Joy (`legends/upper-echelon.md`).
- Goblins say a spark on your boot is "the fire looking for something bigger", and that a barrel
	beside a camp is asking for trouble.
- A dybbuk is said to take hold of someone with a debt left unpaid to its family.
