# Kalle

## Where it is

At -100, 100: south-east of Midworld, a hundred steps out, alone on its arm of the cross (`world/overview.md`).
Its land is the Diggings (`lands/diggings.md`): spoil heaps and red-brown scree, timbered mine
mouths, rusted rails, ruined shrines. Hills and high country rise round it, with a cold pass above
and a bog below.

## What it is known for

- **Mines and miners.** Old mines hold rubies; ice pits go down into winter ice; the mine captains
	sell war picks to their own men for the roads.
- **Blasting powder.** Kalle's miners buy it "by the keg" and haul it up to the diggings in the same
	cart as their ropes and their bread. Each has a story about a keg that went off beside a cook fire
	(`things/battlefield-objects.md`). Kalle, Narcissa and Gilead sell it to anyone with the gold.
- **The pit fights**: gladiators drink Strength Tonic before them; the pit master sells it at cost
	"a dull fight pays him less".
- **Cold, and the storms it sells against.** The passes above it hold snow for half the year. No storm
	comes this far south-east (`world/weather-and-time.md`), but Kalle's wire men and charm-sellers trade on
	the lightning of the storm road to the north-west.
- **A court of the dead.** The Liche King's court is said to meet in the crypts beneath the town.

## Who lives here

- **Miners and mine captains**: wear rime rings under their gloves, frost charms down the ice pits, and
	swear by Frostbite when a cave bear wanders in from the back passages. They trade rubies for bread and
	boots at the first market they reach. A cold-smith works the mine's winter ice pits, and a seam under
	the ice yields the iron of the rime rings; the men who dig it "do not say much about the seam".
- **Armourers and smiths**: sell Ironclad Plate ("a man in it needs a rope to get up from the ground")
	and Lamellar Armor, laced to fit in an afternoon, and furriers sew Rimefur Coats from the pelts of
	the high country.
- **Cutters and charmers**: cut the flame charm's red stones; the charmers sell viper charms to
	travellers bound for the bogs ("every one of them came from the same belt"), and Kalle's apothecaries
	will not sell one without a tin of Antidote.
- **Wire men and apothecaries**: wire men wear spark rings out on the storm road to the north-west, where
	the lightning comes down; the apothecaries grind the same crystal as the Storm Charm into Storm Ward Draught, and Kalle sellers say the crystal
	hums louder when a strike is close.
- **The pan-men** dig a bitter white root at night and mix it with salt from the pans at the mouth of
	the road into Weakening Dust.
- **Soldiers, mercenaries and trainers**: Warriors say a man who will not step back never needs healing
	in time (Stand Fast); trainers warn that Fury's rage stays after the blood has gone; mercenaries use
	Maim "as a sort of shout". Old soldiers keep a hellhound's tooth as a charm.
- **Mages** teach Cold Snap in the cold months, "when their students have the most practice at being
	miserable", and call Angel's Love a fraud, and wear it under their robes.
- **Old wives, farmers and innkeepers**: wives leave a lamp burning in the window against shades;
	farmers bar their doors and keep the dogs in when the moon is full; innkeepers on the road out pay a
	priest to walk the rooms with salt before they let a bed go. Gamblers wear wakeful rings through a
	whole game of cards. See `peoples/trades.md`.

## The market

Inn: 40 gold. It sells the maps of Bris and Narcissa; Kalle's own map costs 100 gold; travel here
costs 30 Travel Points.

- **Weapons**: Hatchet, Axe, Battle Axe, Dark Axe, Poleaxe, War Pick, Frost Staff.
- **Armor**: Chain Mail, Lamellar Armor, Bronze Plate, Iron Plate, Ironclad Plate, Rimefur Coat.
	**Shields**: Buckler, Small Shield, Spiked Targe.
- **Accessories**: Rime Ring, Spark Ring, Traveller's Ring, Wakeful Ring, Lucky Charm, Flame Charm,
	Frost Charm, Storm Charm, Viper Charm.
- **Consumables**: Herb, Antidote, Potion, Hi-Potion, Regen Potion, Ether, Fire Flask, Sleep Powder,
	Acid Vial, Explosive Barrel, Strength Tonic, Weakening Dust, Fire, Frost and Storm Ward Draughts,
	Travel Tea.

New here: the heavy armour of the road (plate and mail), the axes, the Dark Axe, the elemental
charms, the Storm Ward Draught, the Explosive Barrel and the Weakening Dust. The lucky charm's clover is
said to come from fields sunk in the marshes near Kalle, which is why it is dear.

## The land around it

The Diggings (`lands/diggings.md`): spoil, scree, mine mouths and hills, with more hills and peaks than
anywhere (`lands/waters-and-heights.md`). Beyond the pit are ruined shrines (their dark-blue stones are
the mages' poor focus: the Magic Rock) and huts. Below the town lies a bog where thornwood puts out
spines to keep the bears off, and its bark is stripped for rain cloaks (`lands/thornbog.md`). Salt pans
mark the mouth of the road. A forest road runs on past it, wooded slopes lead toward Narcissa, and
hill roads and passes climb above. Cliffs near Kalle give up the Dragon's Scale. No storm comes
here: the passes are frost and wind (`fields/peaks.lua`).

## Monsters

At the gate, levels 19 to 21: Dawk, Figment, Nisse, Hellhound, Uruk, Wizard, Dawkin, Grey Bear,
Megawraith. By lore Kalle's own are:

- **Shades** in the ruined huts north of it after dark (3), **Nightshade** in the glades (6),
	**Figments** in the shuttered houses at night (19), a **Polgergeist** in the inns on the road out (25).
- **Wolves and weres**: Dark Wolves on the forest road (17), Werewolves round the farms (17), Werebears
	on the slopes toward Narcissa (26), Lycanthors in the hill country (28) (`monsters/beasts.md`).
- **Dead soldiers**: a Megaskeleton dragging its cart on the old roads (13), an Armor Knight on the
	barracks road east (33), a Pale Knight bleached white in the snow-fields (39), a Liche King in the
	crypts (44) (`monsters/undead.md`).
- **A Blue Dragon** in the peaks, where snow lies on the road half the year (39), and **the
	Insurgent** with his farmers and smiths on the hill roads (46) (`legends/upper-echelon.md`).
- **Scoundrels** work the roads out of Kalle, selling false maps to new arrivals (12).

## Rumours and habits

- The Liche King "counts every coin himself", and sends servants to collect the tithe.
- The Pale Knight is thought dead by most soldiers in the town. He is not.
- Dragon's Scale is "shed by a young dragon on the cliffs near Kalle".
- Hellhounds on the hot road toward Bris are "faster than any horse", so the advice is to fight from a
	doorway.
