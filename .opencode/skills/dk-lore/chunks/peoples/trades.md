# Trades, guilds and habits

Ordinary people doing ordinary work, and the small habits the work gives them. Everything here is
as the lore of items, fields and monsters has it; the towns' own chunks say what each town sells.

## Farm, field and forest

- Farmers and their children: the Midlands and the Copper Downs. Boys carry reed shields on the first day of
	militia drill and a sickle to the road when the harvest is in and the coins run low; farmers on the Midworld road
	hang a wolf's fang by the door; farmers set traps for critters; farmers round Kalle bar their
	doors on nights of the full moon; the farmers round Roma and Bris "would not know what to do with a dry year".
- Drovers (the yards out of Roma), shepherds (the downs, the Greymoor's stones, Hambry's high pastures) and
	herders (the passes beyond Kalle, the high valleys beyond Endworld). Shepherds count their flocks twice
	a day since the greater wyverns came, and carry a bell.
- Woodcutters and hunters of the Weald and the Thornbog: they mark stumps, stack cut wood by the road, strip
	thornwood bark, will not work a snag in a gale. Honey-cutters of Hambry walk under the hives each morning with
	a smoking pot on a string. Beekeepers east of Bris sell hornet stings for a penny.
- Trappers and furriers: trappers (three bottles of Frost Ward Draught before a winter crossing, two
	come back), Kalle's winter pelts and Rimefur Coats, Gilead's furriers (they pay well for dark bear hides, and a
	grey bear's pelt sells well too), Hambry's hunters (ogre-hide trophies).
- Tanners (Midworld's leather armor, "every tanner in Midworld makes it"), tailors (Midworld's clothes), bakers
	(Trail Snacks in wax paper), snake-catchers (Roma's jewellers make their rings), herbwives and herbalists.
- Stilt-hut folk of the Thornbog leave a loaf on the path for the hag and test each step with a pole.

## Shore and water

- Pearl divers of the Shoals work with a rope and a knife from stakes in the sand; the shallow beds are picked
	clean every spring. Fishermen of Bris cut tortoise shell into squares; their fishwives say a splash is the
	cheapest cure for a burning cloak. Sailors buy Bris's acid for barnacles. Boatmen over the lake beyond
	Endworld leave a lantern in the bow and do not look down. Fishers near Hambry know the drakefins.

## Mine, forge and kiln

- **Kalle's miners**: they dig rubies, ride the ore carts, light their fuses with a taper and count under their
	breath, wear Rime Rings under their gloves and Frost Charms down the ice pits, and go after cave bears with Frost
	Staves from a cold-smith. Mine captains sell War Picks to their own men for the roads. Pan-men dig a bitter white
	root at night for Weakening Dust. Cutters cut the flame stones. Wire men wear spark rings out on the storm road to the north-west, where the lightning comes down.
- **Armourers and smiths**: Kalle (Ironclad Plate, Lamellar Armor, cheaper than plate), Bris (Rime Rings by weight,
	Spiked Targes, a smith "who sells the rings"), Hambry (Greatswords for the city's paid guards), Narcissa (Cinder
	Blades quenched in brine in furnaces never let go out; Warhammers sold to men "who have already decided to take
	the hits"), Gilead (Sunsteel Mail, perhaps one a year, to men who have already been hit by something that
	mattered; Diamonds set into plate; Dawn Maces from the silver of a sunken bell), Midworld (copper rings,
	wands carved from fallen ash), Endworld (Starsteel, from a meteor).
- **Potters and kilns**: Narcissa's Cinder Shields, fired in the kiln that fires its roof tiles.
- **Masons**: Endworld's pile rubble on steam vents. Obsidian cutters knap black glass into blades.
- **Storm-watchers** of Endworld string copper wire down lances and hang them on towers for three or four storms.
- The old mines beneath the hills forge the dark iron blades, axes and armor; few armourers put their mark on
	them (the Dark Sword's lore).

## Glass, copper and clocks

- Roma: copper workers (Spark Rods), glassmen (Ember Rings, lamp beads), clockmakers (Wakeful Rings: "it was a
	clock spring before it was a ring"), tinkers, jewellers, ironmongers.
- Narcissa: glass-cutters (Frost Charms for people who have been north) and a glassmaker who works only after
	dark (Shade Charms from stone out of a cave under the north ice).
- Gilead: glassmiths (Glass Blades) and glassblowers (Void Flasks, from sand "that the miners bring up from below
	the salt marsh").
- The lamplighters' guild sells Fire Flasks "at a price that is never quite explained".

## Healing and herbs

- Apothecaries: Antidote from Midworld to Kalle (Narcissa and on sell Cure-All instead) and Potion; Bris's big apothecary
	(Hi-Potion in a dozen on contract to caravan guards, who sign for each bottle), Bris's chemist (Acid Vial; wears gloves
	to the market) and Bris's apothecaries (Fire Ward Draught); Kalle's (Storm Ward Draught; they will not sell the viper
	charm without a tin of Antidote).
- Healers and surgeons: Hambry's surgeons (Lifeward Shield), Narcissa's old infirmary (the Bitter Draught, from a wide
	clay jug), the abbey of Hambry (Cure-All, first made for monks who came back from the marsh), Bris's clerics (who teach
	Life for free), Gilead's one workshop (Elixir, "the family's motto is painted over the door, and the price is painted
	over the motto").
- Herbalists burn nightshade on purpose; Hambry's keep bottles in the back rooms the town guard would rather not count.

## Learning and magic

- **The Mages' guild** is one body, seated in Roma: it sells apprentice robes, buys magic stones in bulk and every
	thornwood root out of the bog, and its halls sell ward charms in pairs. Narcissa's college stills Hi-Ether in copper on the north
	wall ("the smell set off too many fires in the old lodgings"), keeps fire-mages with oil cups on their staves, and sells
	to nobody it does not know. Hambry's mages buy ward shields for their road guards. Midworld's old masters teach in its
	towers; its apprentices carve wands.
- Scholars of Roma pay for Memory Drops; a monk in the hills above Hambry grinds Sage's Lenses at cost; the judges of
	Narcissa keep Angel's Truths for disputes; scholars in Gilead say Nightmare was written by a mage who could not sleep.
- The dead school beyond Narcissa teaches by fire; its pupils fight their exams (monsters/undead.md).

## The watch, the law and the faith

- Guards and wardens: Midworld's road guards are taught Ward before they get a spear; the gate guards of Narcissa drink Guard
	Tonic before a night watch; Hambry's watch guild gives each man a Sentinel Charm the night he first stands a gate;
	Gilead's garrison buys them by the dozen and holds a gate with Tower Shields; Endworld's wardens wear them on the chest
	because "a sleeping man out there is a dead one"; Roma's guards say to stay upwind with Sleep Powder.
- The pleaders' hall of Hambry sits in judgement "on anyone who comes in without a receipt" (monsters/people.md).
- Priests: Midworld's chapel keeps the Order's oath book and a page for every field held; Hambry's temple blesses maces and
	keeps a grim book on haunts; Gilead's temple sells Vigil Cuirasses and Halo Charms; Bris's priests sell a blessing against
	spirits and call Tithe "the cheapest mana in the realm"; Roma's bless silver shields for a fee; Narcissa's priests say a
	spirit is someone who has not finished their business; bog priests of Gilead carve thornwood hearts.

## Market and road

Carters, caravan drivers and guards, merchants, innkeepers, gamblers, gladiators and minstrels: see peoples/roads-and-outlaws.md.
Gladiators in Kalle drink Strength Tonic at the start of the pit fights and the pit master sells it at cost "because a dull
fight pays him less".

## Habits

- Miners leave a lamp burning at the cave mouth (against dawks); old wives of Kalle leave one in the window (against
	shades).
- Bris's smiths keep a barrel of water by the forge door; Roma's washerwomen keep a bucket by the door.
- Cellar keepers salt the stairs against dark slimes; mages keep a charm of salt by the bed against the nightmare.
- Carters wait at the crossroads for the Narcissa fog to lift; they paint wagons with pitch against wyverns; caravan drivers
	shake out their boots every morning and mark mirages on their maps.
- The people of Endworld tie a wet cloth over the mouth before a fight; scouts carry wet sacks.
- The Hambry guards ring a bell at the gate in a blizzard so the lost can find the road; towns near Narcissa keep a bell-ringer for demons.
- Hunters leave a little salt meat on a rock for a giant's goodwill; folk near the Thornbog leave a loaf for the hag; farmers
	leave a bowl of milk for the nisse and are alarmed when they stop.
- Innkeepers pay a priest to walk the rooms with salt before a bed goes out (Kalle road); merchants of Roma keep their coin in
	locked chests and pay the guards to watch the stables.
