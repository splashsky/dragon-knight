# Mages

"In the towers of Midworld the old masters still teach the words that bend the world: a whisper
that closes wounds, a cry that calls down fire." Mages are frail in a brawl, and every adventurer
they have saved will tell you so. Few of them mind. They start with 15 hit points, 5 magic points,
and the one spell every apprentice learns first: Magic Bolt, a spark of pure will.

They level fast, with high hit points and high magic and low strength and dexterity. Where the
schools are:

- **Midworld**: the towers and the old masters (Torrent, Gust, Whiteout).
- **Roma**: the guild and its apprentices (Cinder Lash, Rain Call, the robes).
- **Narcissa**: the college and the foundry mages (Sap Mind, Sunfire, Hi-Ether, and a Regen they
	took from watching the town's cats).
- **Hambry**: the Mages of Hambry and the wind-readers (Rockfall, Starfall, Barrier, Hail, Cyclone, Gale).
- **Kalle**: Cold Snap taught in the cold months, Frostbite against cave bears.
- **Gilead**: the scholars (Chaos in the deep towns, Nightmare).
- **Endworld**: the oldest masters, who say Inferno was never meant for a fight but for clearing
	roads (Lightning Lance, Renewal, Rend).

The Mages' guild buys every Thornwood Root that comes out of the bog.

## The first lessons

- **Magic Bolt** (level 1). "Before the words that heal or burn, every apprentice learns to throw a
	spark of pure will." It stings more than it wounds.
- **Heal** (2). "The first word every young Mage learns, and the last one a wise one forgets."
- **Hurt** (4) and **Spark** (6). "Every Mage has singed a finger on this one." It costs less than a
	cup of water.
- **Sleep** (7), **Revive** (9), **Enfeeble** (10): the quiet tricks. Enfeeble is a grandmother's trick
	learned from books.
- A Mage's kit: a **Wand** from Midworld, an Apprentice Robe and a Spark Rod from Roma, magic stones
	and Ethers (`things/gear.md`, `things/consumables.md`).

## The ladder

Healing, the one kind school: Heal 2, Revive 9, Life 25, Breath 45,
Renewal 65, Gaia 70. Gaia draws the land's green strength into the body (works best outdoors,
smells of crushed grass); Renewal is "what a Mage learns once Gaia has stopped feeling like a
miracle and started feeling like a bill".

The hurts (arcane, armor ignored; shapes of dark and light are immune, `magic/elements.md`):
Magic Bolt 1, Hurt 4, Pain 12, Sap Mind 29 ("the thief's spell": the bolt takes a little of what the monster
was using to keep itself going, and gives some magic to the caster), Maim 31, Rend 51, Starfall 76 (cannot be bought in a shop; needs a
clear sky and a full turn), Chaos 79 ("written by a Mage or a madman").

The elements, one by one:
- Lightning: Spark 6, Lightning Lance 62 (the Mages of Endworld fire it down the long road, then sit
	on the stones for a turn while their heads stop ringing).
- Frost: Frostbite 16, Hail 46, Whiteout 100 ("the last thing an old Mage learns and the first thing a
	sensible one stops casting in a closed room").
- Fire: Ember 19, Cinder Lash 23, Scorch 45, Inferno 88 ("the haystack, the barn and the field behind it").
- Water: Torrent 24. Wind: Gust 22, Cyclone 52. Earth: Rockfall 42 (paid in the caster's blood: the
	masters will not teach it to anyone who cannot afford the bruises).

The weather a Mage can hire: Rain Call 20, Sunfire 26, Cold Snap 28, Gale 32, Storm Call 41. A Mage who
calls a storm does not choose who is struck, "and the old hands say that is the first thing to learn".

The wards and the hold:
- Sleep 7, Dream 17, Nightmare 40 (costs dearly; the Mages who use it rarely sleep well).
- Ward 14, Fend 36, Barrier 60; Fire Ward 34, Frost Ward 37, Storm Ward 38.
- Poison and drain: Venom 33, Withering 54 (a Mage who lives on other people's blood "is still a Mage, but a thin one"), Curse 73, Plague 83 ("Gaia's priests in
	Hambry will not teach it").
- Allies: Call Spirit 35 (the priests of Narcissa say a spirit is someone who has not finished), Raise
	Golem 94 (`magic/allies.md`).
- Support: Wellspring 21 (taught over a bad cup of tea), Cleanse 27, Tithe 57.

## What people say

- Mages of Midworld say wizards are no true mages; the wizards take it poorly (`monsters/people.md`).
- Innkeepers in Bris pay for Dream; innkeepers in Midworld say Sleep works as well on drunk customers.
- Apothecaries sell the potions and draughts for those who have not learned the spell
	(`things/consumables.md`).
- Mages dislike Thornwood Bark, "since it seems to pull the magic out of the air around it".
