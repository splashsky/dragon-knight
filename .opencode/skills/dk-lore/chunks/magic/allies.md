# Allies

A caster does not make a friend; it borrows one for a few rounds, by a promise, a horn, a thank you.
Each ally fights beside you for a few turns and then leaves, and a monster may strike it instead of
you. Nobody keeps an ally: "it never lingers after the fight is done."

None of the monsters uses them: the knights, liches and Paladin-monsters all have the spells switched
off.

## The four

- **Bear** (Call Bear, 12 magic; Warrior 24, Paladin 27). A brown bear from the woods north of Midworld,
	called out of the trees with a horn and a few words the caster would rather not repeat. Bears come
	down from the hills above Roma in autumn looking for honey and not for trouble; the caster
	promises it something it wants more than the fight. Slow to tire, hits very hard (80 life,
	attack 22), "more bad-tempered than hungry, which is some comfort". It stays about four rounds
	and lumbers back into the trees.
- **Wolf** (Call Wolf, 10 magic; learned by no class). A grey wolf that answers the call from the edge
	of the road and runs at the caster's side until the fight is done. Not tame, no interest in being
	petted; quick, fragile (30 life, attack 12). Hunters from Roma say it always leaves before the
	trouble does; hunters in the hills near Kalle claim it will follow anyone who calls it properly.
	Five rounds, then it "runs off into the wild".
- **Spirit** (Call Spirit, 20 magic; Mage 35, Paladin 41). The restless shade of someone who died with
	unfinished business, bound for a few turns. Quick and hard to pin down (defense 40, dexterity 30;
	a blade passes through it), courteous "in the way of the dead, which is to say rarely"; its touch
	leaves a chill on the skin. Six rounds. Mages thank it properly after, since a spirit that feels
	ignored tends to wander; the priests of Narcissa say a spirit is what is left of a person who has
	not finished their business.
- **Golem** (Raise Golem, 40 magic; Mage 94, Warrior 64, Paladin 68). A figure of stone, raised from
	the old foundations of a broken chapel and bound to serve the one who speaks its name. It is
	slow to wake and slow to turn, strikes seldom and hard (450 life, attack 90, defense 100), and
	stands in front of its caster without being asked. The priests say the stone remembers what it was
	built to guard. A golem that is not thanked seems to take longer to rise the next time. Paladins
	use it to hold a line. Four rounds, then it crumbles back into rubble.

## Habits of calling

- Thank the ally. Every account of the spirit and the golem says so.
- Hunters tell of wolves and bears; priests tell of spirits and golems.
