# Warriors

"Warriors learn their trade on the long road between Midworld and Roma, where the slimes are many
and the inns are few. They trust steel over spells, and a good night's rest over both. When the
monsters of the far lands come down from the hills, it is a Warrior who stands at the gate."

Medium hit points, high strength, low magic and low dexterity; they level at a middling pace. They
start with 15 hit points, no magic points, and no spell at all. Most of what they know is **shouts,
strikes and stubbornness**; they learn only a little magic, and little of it is gentle.

## Where it is taught

- **Midworld's training yards**: Cleave, with a wooden sword and a bucket of sand so the sergeants
	could see where the blow landed. Revive is the first thing young Warriors are told to learn.
- **Roma's yards**: Mire (the mud is kept fresh for the purpose), Craze (the taverns), Blood Strike
	(old hands who say the blood is the point). Roma's priests bless silver shields.
- **Kalle**: Stand Fast ("a man who will not step back never needs healing in time"), Fury (the
	trainers who warn the rage stays), Hunter's Mark (hunters of the hills above Kalle chalk a sign
	on the track).
- **The northern road**: the reavers, whose trick Reaver is.
- **The sergeants everywhere**: War Cry, Second Wind, Last Stand, Warlord, Whirlwind (the yard
	masters at 44).

## The ladder

- **Cries**: War Cry 3, Craze 18, Rage 38, Fury 70, Warlord 100. Each makes the next blow harder.
	War Cry is "nothing but breath and noise"; Warlord is for a handful of men who do not like the
	name, a voice that carries across a muddy field in a gale and makes frightened farmers take
	one more step. Craze is a cheap trick; Rage is "the spell fighters learn when Craze stops being
	enough".
- **Strikes** (neutral, armor ignored): Cleave 6, Blood Strike 20 (costs your own blood, a bad trade
	with no healer in reach), Reaver 28 (heals you for half of the blow), Whirlwind 44 (every
	monster and object on the field), Finishing Blow 60 (twice as hard on a bleeding monster; "the
	mercy stroke"). Hurt 5 and Pain 14 are borrowed from the Mages.
- **Holding**: Grit 10 (below half life only), Stand Fast 30 (blood for a firmer guard), Second Wind
	36 ("mostly stubbornness"), Last Stand 42 (the pass where forty men held a road for a night and a
	day, and most of them are still buried), Fend 45, Barrier 58.
- **Marking**: Stun 17 ("not sleep, whatever the mages say"), Hunter's Mark 40 (weapons land harder
	on the marked), Enfeeble 48, Venom 82, Curse 94.
- **Ground and field**: Mire 21 (the Warrior's friend), Rockfall 76. Earth hits harder in mud, and
	everyone is clumsier.
- **Healing**: Heal 2, Revive 11, Life 29, Wellspring 54, Cleanse 88; Tithe 32 (blood for mana).
- **Sleep and allies**: Sleep 8, Dream 22, Nightmare 51; Call Bear 24, Raise Golem 64
	(`magic/allies.md`).

## What people say

- "Trust steel over spells."
- Knights and soldiers in `monsters/people.md` fight as Warriors: they open with a war cry, raise a
	standard, stand fast when hurt, and last-stand under a quarter.
- A Warrior's gear is the weapon, armor and shield ladders (`things/gear.md`); Gladiators in Kalle
	drink Strength Tonic before the pit fights (`things/consumables.md`).
