# The lore's chunks

One line per chunk: its id (the path under `chunks/` without `.md`) and what it holds.
`scripts/check.py` requires every chunk here once.

## The world

- `world/overview`: The map: axes, the eight towns and the cross they lie on, distance as danger, the road order, the edge, and how to read a place named in lore.
- `world/weather-and-time`: The two-hour day, the storms and their road from Midworld to Gilead, the climates and snow lines, and what the weather fields say.

## Towns

- `towns/midworld`: Midworld, the hub town where every character begins: its market, towers and chapel, and the quiet Midlands round it.
- `towns/roma`: Roma, the first town out of Midworld: hill roads and goblins, copper, glass and clocks, the Mages' guild, rain and mud.
- `towns/bris`: Bris, the shoal-town of the north-west: pearl divers, smiths and apothecaries, cliffs with young dragons, the storms' road.
- `towns/kalle`: Kalle, the mining town of the south-east: pits and ice pits, miners and gladiators, blasting powder, cold passes and crypts.
- `towns/narcissa`: Narcissa, the walled foundry-town of the south-west moor: fog at dawn, kilns and fire-mages, and the dead school beyond.
- `towns/hambry`: Hambry, the snowbound forest city of the north-east: abbey, temple and watch guild, the hall of the pleaders, the fire pits below.
- `towns/gilead`: Gilead, the walled garrison-town of the north-west barrens where the storms end: glassmiths, temple smiths, wardens, battlefields beyond.
- `towns/endworld`: Endworld, the last town, in the ash at the south-west corner: legendary gear for sale, wardens who do not sleep, the edge behind it.

## Lands

- `lands/midlands`: The Midlands, Midworld's farmland: hedgerows and stubble, no battlefield field, the weather's plain numbers, the first monsters, and no objects.
- `lands/copper-downs`: The Copper Downs round Roma: wheat-coloured hills and chalk tracks, goblin camps and campfires, rain and slow mud, copper, glass and clocks.
- `lands/salt-shoals`: The Salt Shoals round Bris: grey tidal flats, shell beds and pearl divers, cliffs and ruined towers, sea mist, tide pools.
- `lands/diggings`: The Diggings round Kalle: spoil heaps, red scree, timbered mine mouths and ice pits, dry bright weather, the high country above, and blasting powder.
- `lands/greymoor`: The Greymoor round Narcissa: heather, peat water and standing stones, the foggiest dawns, old battlefields, foundry smoke and the dead school.
- `lands/hambry-weald`: The Hambry Weald: close forest in the north-east, moss and bear sign, honey and woodcutters, fog off the marsh-forest, snow half the year, beehives.
- `lands/storm-barrens`: The Storm Barrens round Gilead: ruined walls and old battlefields, endless wind, flooded mines and salt flats, harder storms, war banners and dead trees.
- `lands/ashlands`: The Ashlands at the world's end round Endworld: grey ash, black glass, lava vents, daily heat, no snow, cinders on the battlefield and steam vents.
- `lands/dry-country`: The Dry Country, the wild sand-and-scrub land between the towns: salt pans, dry wells, nomads, scorpions, mirages, wyverns, the sand battlefield and water cacti.
- `lands/thornbog`: The Thornbog, the wild bog-land: black water, reeds and thornwood, stilt-huts and hags, the wettest ground, gas pockets, and the old marsh dragons.
- `lands/waters-and-heights`: Lakes, ponds, hills and peaks across every land: where they lie, their battlefield fields, who lives in them, and how near each town stands to water and height.

## Peoples and trades

- `peoples/trades`: The crafts, guilds, workers and habits of the world's people: farm and forest, shore, mine and forge, glass and clocks, healing, learning, the watch and the faith.
- `peoples/roads-and-outlaws`: Travellers, caravans and inns on the roads, and the people who prey on them: bandits, thieves, tollmen, deserters and the unpaid garrisons.

## Magic and the classes

- `magic/elements`: The eleven kinds of harm (physical, fire, frost, lightning, water, wind, earth, holy, shadow, arcane, poison): what each is in the world, its spells and gear, its weather, who fears it.
- `magic/mages`: The Mage's tradition: the towers and guilds that teach it, what each school of spell is like, and the ladder from Magic Bolt to Whiteout.
- `magic/warriors`: The Warrior's tradition: the training yards, the sergeants' cries and strikes, and the ladder from War Cry to Warlord.
- `magic/paladins`: The Paladin's oath and prayers: the Midworld chapel, the temples that teach them, and the ladder from Mend to Dawn.
- `magic/allies`: The four summoned allies (bear, wolf, spirit, golem): where each is borrowed from, who calls it, what it is like to fight beside.
- `magic/afflictions`: The states a fight leaves on people (burning, poison, sleep, doom, wards, marks, rage), as the world knows them, and what cures them.

## Monsters

- `monsters/others`: The monsters of the sea beyond Endworld, and the rules every monster family shares: where levels are met, resistances, tells, who never roams.
- `monsters/slimes`: The slimes: blue, red and dark jelly from the cellars and roads, and the rare silver one that carries coin.
- `monsters/beasts`: The animals of the wild: small beasts and ravens, scorpions, wolves and lycans, bears, great hunters, the hell-kin and the walking plant.
- `monsters/drakes-and-dragons`: Drakes, wyrms, wyverns and the six dragons: where each kind lairs, what it fears, and the tell before its breath or dive.
- `monsters/undead`: The walking dead and the things of dread: skeletons, ghouls, wraiths, the five liches and their two courts, and the fright and doomer kin.
- `monsters/fey-and-spirits`: Ghosts and the restless dead, shadows, illusions and dream-things, and the small fey of ponds, wind and barns.
- `monsters/demons`: Imps and the devils of the pits, the demons of the realm beyond the cracks and their court, the four daemons, Lucifuge, and the tempters.
- `monsters/elementals`: The elementals of fire, earth, wind and water: what shaped them, where they gather, and the weather each one calls.
- `monsters/giants`: The big folk: orcs and uruks, the dawk and brute kin, trolls, ogres, giants, the Titan and the Colossus.
- `monsters/people`: The people who fight you: bandits and cheats, deserters, the old garrison's knights, casters, and the pleaders of Hambry's hall.

## Things

- `things/gear`: The weapon, armor, shield and charm ladders from a Midworld stick to Endworld steel: who makes each rung, where it is sold, what it costs the wearer.
- `things/consumables`: The things a traveller drinks, eats and throws: herbs to elixirs, draughts and tonics, flasks and dusts, who brews them and where they are sold.
- `things/legendary-sets`: What monsters leave behind, from the Life Pebble to the gems, and the sets: Destiny, Dragon's, Angel's, Seraph's, Demon's and Thornwood.
- `things/battlefield-objects`: The ten objects found on the battlefield (barrel, hive, gas pocket, steam vent, standing stone, dead tree, tide pool, war banner, campfire, water cactus): their lands, what folk say of them, who uses them, and the wind.

## Legends

- `legends/upper-echelon`: The legendary: dragons and the demon host, the dead and the crown's old servants, the Destiny gear and four accessory sets, the late spells, and the old story.
- `legends/sayings`: Proverbs, rumours and habits of speech that recur in the lore: who says what, what folk do against what, and the recurring motifs.
