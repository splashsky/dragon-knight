A field is an effect on the whole battlefield, for everyone in the fight. The weather where you stand brings its own: the sky (sun, heat, fog, rain, a downpour, a thunderstorm, snow, a blizzard), the ground (mud, ice) and the night. They follow the weather as it changes during a fight. Some spells call up a field for a while in place of the weather's (rain on a clear day, say), and auras come only from spells. One field of each group is up at a time, and the fight page shows them under the monster.
