Objects stand on the battlefield beside the fighters: they have hit points and can be hit, burnt and set off, but never take a turn, and a fight is won or lost on the monsters and you alone. Some turn up by themselves where the land suits them, never near Midworld; others are carried in and placed, by you or by a monster. A spell that hits everything on the field hits them too. With more than one thing to aim at, the fight page lets you pick your target.
