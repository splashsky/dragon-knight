  • Healing spells (Heal, Revive, Life, Breath, Gaia) restore up to their power in hit points, until your current HP is full.
  • Hurt spells (Hurt, Pain, Maim, Rend, Chaos) deal between five sixths of their power and their power in damage to the monster, regardless of the monster's armor.
  • Sleep spells (Sleep, Dream, Nightmare) put the monster to sleep. The monster has a power in 15 chance of remaining asleep each turn.
  • Damage spells (Craze, Rage, Fury) increase your total attack damage by their power in percent until the end of the fight.
  • Defense spells (Ward, Fend, Barrier) reduce the total damage you take from the monster by their power in percent until the end of the fight.
